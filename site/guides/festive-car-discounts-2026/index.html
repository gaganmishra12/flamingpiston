Festive Car Discounts in October 2026: What You Actually Save

A dealer says you can save ₹1 lakh. You ask for the final quotation and the number suddenly becomes ₹35,000. Usually, the missing amount has not vanished: it depended on exchanging a car, qualifying for a corporate scheme or accepting something you did not plan to buy.

October is a good time to compare offers carefully, not a reason to stop doing the maths. The headline should be the beginning of the conversation. Your final payable amount is the useful result.

The Rule That Keeps This Simple

Ask for two numbers: the car’s price without exchanging your old car, and the separate amount offered for that old car. Then ask which discounts you qualify for without taking dealer finance or accessories.

Break the Offer into Its Parts

Manufacturer offer pages show why the details matter. Renault’s published terms separate cash, exchange, corporate and loyalty benefits, with conditions tied to variants, regions and evidence of eligibility. An “up to” total is therefore not automatically available to every buyer. [1]

BenefitQuestion to ask
Cash discountDoes this reduce the invoice for my exact variant?
Exchange bonusIs it additional to the independently quoted value of my old car?
Loyalty or corporate offerWhat documents establish eligibility?
AccessoriesWould I otherwise buy these, and at what price?
Insurance or finance benefitWhat changes in cover, interest, fees or conditions?

A ₹1 Lakh Headline, Worked Through

Consider an illustrative offer: ₹35,000 cash, ₹30,000 exchange bonus, ₹15,000 corporate benefit and ₹20,000 of accessories. These add to ₹1 lakh. A buyer with no exchange car and no corporate eligibility receives ₹35,000 in cash benefit plus the accessory bundle. Calling that a ₹1 lakh cash saving would be wrong.

Now suppose the dealer values your old car ₹25,000 below an independent buyer’s written offer. The ₹30,000 exchange bonus improves that position by only ₹5,000. Compare the net result, not the word “bonus”. All figures in this example are invented to explain the calculation; they are not an advertised offer for a particular car.

Make Two Quotations Comparable

Get the same variant, transmission, colour and manufacturing period on both sheets. Ask for the ex-showroom price, registration, insurance, optional equipment and every discount on separate lines. A quote with expensive insurance and accessories can carry the larger discount while still costing more.

For finance, compare the total scheduled repayment and fees as well as the monthly instalment. A longer term can lower the instalment without reducing the cost. Ask the lender for the repayment schedule; do not reconstruct the deal from a salesperson’s rounded monthly number.

Check the Actual Car Before the Deadline

A useful discount is attached to a car you want in an acceptable condition. Ask for the allocated vehicle’s identity, manufacturing information and an inspection opportunity before the final purchase steps. Check the body, tyres, glass, interior, electronics and supplied documents, and record any agreed rectification in writing.

There is no universal percentage that makes older stock a bargain. The useful questions are whether the specification has changed, whether the condition is satisfactory and whether the price compensates you for anything you are giving up.

Our Take

Choose the lowest comparable cost for the car you actually want, supported by clear written terms. A smaller unconditional cash discount can beat a larger package you cannot fully use. Save the quotation and its validity date: offers can change, and online pages can still display an earlier campaign.

FAQs

Is an exchange bonus the value of my old car?
No. Ask for the old car’s valuation and the additional exchange benefit separately, then compare the net result with other offers.

Are accessories the same as a cash discount?
No. Their useful value depends on whether you want them and what you would otherwise pay.

Does this guide list guaranteed October deals?
No. It explains how to evaluate conditional offers. Verify the exact variant, region, eligibility and validity date in writing.
