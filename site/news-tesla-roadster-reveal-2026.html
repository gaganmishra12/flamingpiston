Tesla Roadster Finally Takes Flight in Musk’s Demo — Production Is Still Planned for 2028

The Tesla Roadster finally did something more useful than collect another promised reveal date: it appeared in a live demonstration. At the 15 October event in Texas — the morning of 16 October in India — Elon Musk showed a car performing thruster-assisted manoeuvres. The production target is now 2028, and a final selling price remains unannounced.

What Actually Changed

There is now a public demonstration to discuss. There is still no customer delivery to review. Those are very different milestones, and treating them as the same thing would miss the most useful part of this story.

The Flying Bit Needs Context

Event coverage describes the Roadster travelling up an inverted ramp, rotating in the air and returning to the ground with assistance from its thrusters. TechCrunch also reported a jump over a line of Tesla vehicles. This was a carefully staged demonstration, not a normal road journey.

Calling that a “flying car” makes a memorable headline. It does not tell a buyer how long the system can operate, how often it can repeat the manoeuvre, or where it may legally be used. Those questions matter more than the height of a single jump.

0–60 mph Is Not 0–100 km/h

Car and Driver reported a claimed 0–60 mph run of about 0.8 seconds during the event. That endpoint is approximately 96.6 km/h, not 100 km/h. The figure is a demonstration claim; FlamingPiston has not independently timed the car.

Before comparing it with another supercar, we would want the timing method, surface conditions, rollout convention and exact vehicle configuration. A spectacular number without those details is a reason to pay attention, not a complete performance comparison.

Three Names, Several Unanswered Questions

Reporting on the updated reservation page identifies Performance, Thrust and an invitation-only Rocket version. Reported deposits of $50,000 and $250,000 are reservation amounts, not confirmed purchase prices. Tesla’s own page says the displayed specifications relate to an optional Thrust augment and that the Rocket trim is not offered in every region.

HeadlineHow to read it
Thruster-assisted demonstrationEvidence of a demonstration, not universal road approval

What Does This Mean for India?

We did not find a confirmed India price or delivery schedule in the sources checked for this update. Converting a dollar deposit into rupees would therefore answer the wrong question. It would not account for the eventual vehicle price, local specification or approval process.

For Indian readers, the Roadster is currently a technology and performance story. It is not yet a car with a verified local buying proposition. Anyone considering a reservation should read the terms for their own region and distinguish refundable amounts from other payments before committing.

Flaming Piston's Take

The demonstration gives the Roadster something its long timeline badly needed: a concrete event rather than another rendering or deadline. The next meaningful evidence will be production specifications, repeatable testing and customer cars. Until then, enjoy the engineering theatre, but keep the purchase decision separate from it.

FAQs

When will the new Tesla Roadster enter production?
Musk stated a 2028 production target at the October 2026 reveal. That is a plan, not a guaranteed delivery date.

Is the Tesla Roadster’s price confirmed?
A final selling price was not announced in the event reporting checked. Reservation deposits must not be treated as the full price.

Can you buy the new Roadster in India?
We have not verified an India price or customer delivery schedule. A global reveal does not establish local availability.
