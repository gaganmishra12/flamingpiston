BMW i5 Long Wheelbase Launched in India — First Locally Made Electric Sedan from BMW Chennai

Image: BMW
G
Gagan Mishra
Published Aug 21, 2026
4 min read
Category: News
Share

BMW India has opened bookings for the i5 Long Wheelbase, making India one of only two countries in the world — after China — where this extended-wheelbase version of the i5 is locally assembled. The car is built at BMW's Chennai facility, which already produces the petrol 5 Series. Adding an electric variant to that line is a meaningful infrastructure commitment, not just a product decision.

The i5 LWB measures 5,175 mm overall — 115 mm longer than the standard i5 — with a 3,105 mm wheelbase, up 110 mm. Every millimetre of that extension goes into rear legroom, which is the point. BMW also fits the LWB with adaptive rear-axle air suspension as standard, something the regular i5 doesn't get, which will matter on variable Indian road surfaces. Under the floor sits an 81.6 kWh battery powering a single rear motor producing 264 horsepower. BMW's MIDC-certified range figure is 669 km.

To mark the launch, BMW India has created a First Edition strictly limited to 99 units. It gets a Rear Seat Entertainment Package, BMW Individual Silver Bronze interior with Fineline open-pore wood and Silver Bronze inlay, a laser-engraved First Edition dashboard panel, Carbon Black Metallic paint, and Titanium Bronze exterior accent detailing. Final retail pricing has not been announced, but local assembly means the i5 LWB avoids the heavy import duty levied on completely built-up vehicles — the same tariff advantage that has kept the locally made X1 LWB and 3 Series competitively positioned relative to their CBU rivals.

Why It Matters

The i5 LWB sits below the i7 in BMW's electric sedan hierarchy — the i7 starts above ₹1.95 crore and measures 5,391 mm — but above the standard i5 M60 xDrive (₹1.19 crore, standard wheelbase). It targets the segment of luxury buyers who want a chauffeur-driven electric sedan with genuine rear-seat space, without paying flagship i7 money. Local assembly makes that case considerably more viable than it would be as a CBU import. The 99-unit First Edition will sell out quickly; broader variant availability and full pricing are expected to follow.

Source: BMW India official announcement.
