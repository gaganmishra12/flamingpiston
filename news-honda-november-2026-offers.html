Honda November 2026 Offers: Up to ₹1.86 Lakh on the City, Nothing on the Elevate

Image: Honda Cars India, edited by FlamingPiston
Gagan Mishra
Published Nov 20, 2026
3 min read
Category: News
Share

Honda is offering benefits of up to ₹1.86 lakh on the City this November, its biggest offer of the month. The third-generation Amaze gets up to ₹66,800 and the older second-generation Amaze up to ₹57,600. The Elevate and ZR-V get no general discount at all. The offers run until 30 November 2026.

What This Means for Buyers

“Up to ₹1.86 lakh” is a ceiling, not a cash price cut. It bundles cash discounts, exchange and loyalty bonuses and corporate offers, and few buyers qualify for all of them. Before you book, get the dealer to write down exactly which parts apply to your variant.

November Offers at a Glance

ModelMaximum benefit (November 2026)
Honda CityUp to ₹1.86 lakh
Amaze (third generation)Up to ₹66,800
Amaze (second generation)Up to ₹57,600
ElevateNo general offer
ZR-VNo general offer

Honda has not published a variant-wise split for November. The benefits can include cash discounts, exchange bonuses, loyalty benefits and corporate offers, and they vary by variant, city and stock. Employees of companies on Honda’s corporate list may still get a corporate offer on every model, including the Elevate and ZR-V.

The City’s Discount Keeps Growing

The City was facelifted in May, yet its maximum benefit has risen every month since September:

Month (2026)City maximum benefit
SeptemberUp to ₹1.30 lakh
OctoberUp to ₹1.71 lakh
NovemberUp to ₹1.86 lakh

Honda has not said why. For a buyer, the trend matters more than the reason: the City’s price is clearly negotiable right now. The City sells from about ₹12 lakh to ₹21 lakh ex-showroom. In September, the largest figures were on the top variants. Expect November to be similar, and do not assume the base SV gets ₹1.86 lakh off.

If the City is on your list, compare it with its closest rival in our Skoda Slavia facelift buyer’s brief.

Why There Is Nothing on the Elevate

In October, the outgoing Elevate carried benefits of up to ₹2.35 lakh. Honda then brought in the updated Elevate, and the November list has no general offer on it. If you see a large Elevate discount at a dealer this month, check the manufacturing date: it may be leftover older stock, which can be worth less at resale.

Five Things to Ask the Dealer

  • Which parts of the “up to” figure apply to my exact variant: cash, exchange, loyalty or corporate?
  • Is the exchange bonus on top of the fair value of my old car, or is it taken out of it?
  • What is the manufacturing month of the car I will get?
  • If the extended warranty is included, how many years does it cover, and is it already on the invoice?
  • Will the offer still apply if delivery slips past 30 November?

FAQs

How much discount is there on the Honda City in November 2026?
Up to ₹1.86 lakh, combining cash, exchange, loyalty and corporate benefits. The full amount does not apply to every variant or buyer.

Is there a discount on the Honda Elevate in November 2026?
No general offer. Only corporate offers for employees of eligible companies may apply.

Till when are Honda’s November offers valid?
Until 30 November 2026.
