A five-star Bharat NCAP rating no longer needs a big budget. Eleven cars that start under ₹10 lakh ex-showroom have been rated five stars for adult protection by India’s official crash-test programme. Here they are, sorted by starting price, with the scores and the detail most lists leave out: which variants the rating actually covers. This list covers petrol, diesel and CNG cars; EVs are in our battery rental guide.

How to Use This List

Start with your budget band, then compare the adult score (out of 32) and child score (out of 49). Check that the rating covers the exact variant you plan to buy, and remember that a car missing from this list may simply never have been tested.

2

Highest Scores in Each Budget Band

Under ₹7 lakh: the Tata Punch has the best scores here, with 30.58 for adults and a full 45 for children. The Altroz is close on child protection and is the hatchback option. The Dzire is the sedan choice in this band.

₹7–8 lakh: the Skoda Kylaq leads with 30.88 and 45. The Brezza scores well for adults at 30.41. The Honda Amaze is the sedan here, with five stars for adults and four for children; the cheaper Dzire scores higher on both counts.

₹8–10 lakh: the Hyundai Venue has the highest adult score on this list at 31.15. The Kia Syros is close behind on both scores.

Differences of a point or less are small. Once a car has five stars, practical things like boot space, gearbox and running cost are a fair way to decide between close scorers.

3

Check the Rating Covers Your Variant

Bharat NCAP tests specific variants and states which versions the rating applies to. For most cars on this list it covers the whole range, but not always:

  • Dzire: the base LXi was tested, and the rating applies to all variants.
  • Punch: CNG variants were tested, and the rating covers the entire line-up.
  • Venue: covers the whole range, including the N Line.
  • Kia Syros: the rating applies to the petrol range.
  • Citroën Aircross: covers the five-seat version’s petrol and turbo-petrol variants.
  • Kylaq, Nexon, Amaze: the applicability is not spelled out in the summary we used. Check the Bharat NCAP report for your variant.

For the XUV 3XO, the CNG version has since been rated separately, with a higher child score of 45. See our XUV 3XO CNG report.

4

Missing From the List Does Not Mean Unsafe

Bharat NCAP tests are voluntary, so manufacturers choose which cars to submit. Several popular cars in this price range had no Bharat NCAP result in Autocar India’s list, which means there is no official Indian score to compare, not that the car has failed.

The Maruti Baleno was tested and received four stars (26.52 adult, 34.81 child), and the Citroën Basalt four stars (26.19 and 35.90). We have left them out of the main table because they are not five-star cars.

For more on how one car’s score is put together, read our Brezza safety explainer.

FAQs

Which is the cheapest 5-star Bharat NCAP car?
On current starting prices, the Tata Punch, from around ₹5.6–5.8 lakh ex-showroom, with 30.58/32 for adults and 45/49 for children.

Which car under ₹10 lakh has the highest Bharat NCAP adult score?
The Hyundai Venue, with 31.15 out of 32, on the results listed here.

Does a 5-star rating apply to every variant?
Not always. Bharat NCAP states which variants a rating covers. Check that it includes the variant you plan to buy.
