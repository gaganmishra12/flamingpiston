Volvo EX90 Listed in India from ₹1.26 Crore

AI illustration: FlamingPiston
Image: Volvo
Gagan Mishra
Published Aug 2026 · Updated Oct 16, 2026
3 min read
Category: News
Share

Volvo now lists the EX90 on its India website from ₹1.26 crore. The old “arriving this month” preview is superseded by a local product listing, though buyers should confirm stock, seating configuration and delivery timing with their retailer.

Volvo’s current India page lists the Twin Motor with up to 456 hp and an estimated range of up to 625 km under WLTP conditions. Volvo labels these figures preliminary, with certification pending; they are not an independently verified road-test result.

The EX90 and XC90 are distinct products: Volvo still lists the XC90 mild hybrid in India. The earlier claim that the EX90 simply replaces an outgoing XC90 on the same platform was misleading and has been removed.

Why It Matters

For buyers, the useful next step is an exact vehicle specification and a home-charging assessment. Check the seating layout, warranty and delivery conditions on the quotation. Do not compare preliminary WLTP range directly with an Indian-cycle claim for another SUV.

Sources checked 16 October 2026: Volvo Cars India EX90 listing; see also the current XC90 listing. Launch prices and claimed specifications are not an independent road test.
