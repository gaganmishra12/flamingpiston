Mahindra XEV 9S Gets a 5-Star Bharat NCAP Rating: How It Compares With the XUV 7XO and Safari

AI illustration: FlamingPiston
Gagan Mishra
Published Nov 21, 2026
4 min read
Category: News
Share

The Mahindra XEV 9S has a five-star Bharat NCAP rating for both adult and child occupant protection. It scored 31.15 out of 32 for adults and 45 out of 49 for children, according to Bharat NCAP’s fact sheet, and Mahindra announced the result on 17 November 2026. No electric seven-seater had reached five stars in India’s crash-test programme before, according to Mahindra.

What This Means for Buyers

The XEV 9S now has the best published Bharat NCAP score of any seven-seater, but its lead over the XUV 7XO and Safari is small. All three are five-star cars. The rating covers every pack and battery size, so focus on what changes between variants: airbags, ADAS and price.

Every adult point the XEV 9S lost was lost in the frontal test. Bharat NCAP judged chest protection for the co-driver “adequate”; all other body regions scored “good”. The side impact score is perfect. For children, it scored 24 out of 24 for how the child dummies fared in the crashes and 12 out of 12 for fitting child seats. All four missing points came from the vehicle assessment, the part of the child score that is not a crash test.

Scores are from Bharat NCAP’s published ratings; the last column simply adds the two together. Mahindra says 76.15 points is the highest combined score of any seven-seater Bharat NCAP has tested so far. On these numbers it leads its own petrol and diesel sibling, the XUV 7XO, by less than half a point, and the Tata Safari by about one and a half. All three are five-star cars. A gap this small should not decide which one you buy. Powertrain, running costs and which features come on your variant matter far more.

It is also the third electric SUV on Mahindra’s INGLO platform to get five stars, after the XEV 9e and the BE 6. If you are weighing the smaller five-seater, our Mahindra BE 6 buying review covers which version to pick.

Which Variants the Rating Covers

Bharat NCAP crash-tested two versions, both with the 79 kWh battery: a Pack Two Above six-seater and a Pack Three Above seven-seater. Its fact sheet then applies the rating to the One Above, Two Above, Three and Three Above packs, across the 59 kWh, 70 kWh and 79 kWh batteries, in both six-seat and seven-seat form. So you do not need the most expensive version to get the five-star structure.

Safety equipment still differs by variant. Bharat NCAP lists six airbags as standard: front, side chest and pelvis airbags for both front occupants, and curtain airbags. Electronic stability control, pedestrian protection and seat-belt reminders for every seat are standard on all variants. The driver’s knee airbag is listed as optional. Autocar India and Rushlane report it on the Pack Three versions, where it takes the count to the seven airbags Mahindra quotes. Level 2+ ADAS, with five radars and a camera, is limited to the top variants.

What to Check Before You Book

  • Does my exact variant have the driver’s knee airbag, or only the six standard airbags?
  • Where will child seats go? ISOFIX mounts are fitted only to the two outer second-row seats. In Bharat NCAP’s child-seat installation check, the third-row outer seats were marked exempt, so the rating tells you nothing about fitting child seats there.
  • Which ADAS functions does my variant have, if any?
  • What will I actually pay? Mahindra currently lists the XEV 9S from ₹21.40 lakh to ₹30.90 lakh ex-showroom, and those prices include its 11.2 kW home charger. Prices have changed since the November 2025 launch, so get the on-road figure for your variant in writing.

FAQs

What is the Mahindra XEV 9S Bharat NCAP rating?
Five stars for both adult and child occupant protection. It scored 31.15 out of 32 for adults and 45 out of 49 for children.

Which XEV 9S variants does the five-star rating cover?
Bharat NCAP applies it to the One Above, Two Above, Three and Three Above packs, with the 59 kWh, 70 kWh and 79 kWh batteries, in six-seat and seven-seat form.

How many airbags does the Mahindra XEV 9S have?
Six airbags are standard. Bharat NCAP lists a driver’s knee airbag as optional, which makes seven where it is fitted.
