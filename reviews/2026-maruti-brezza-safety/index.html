Maruti Brezza Safety & Bharat NCAP Explained

"5-star Bharat NCAP" gets used as a headline number without most articles explaining what it actually measures. Here's what those stars mean, how the Brezza's specific scores stack up, and what safety kit you actually get across the range.

The Brezza's Actual Score

5 stars overall, with a published Adult Occupant Protection (AOP) score of 30.41 out of 32 and a Child Occupant Protection (COP) score of 43 out of 49. For context, the minimum to qualify for 5 stars is 27/32 on AOP and 41/49 on COP, so the Brezza clears both thresholds with genuine margin, not a borderline pass.

What Bharat NCAP Actually Tests

TestSpeedWhat it simulates
Frontal offset impact64 km/hHead-on collision
Side impact50 km/hT-bone collision from the side
Side pole impact29 km/hCollision with a pole or tree

Cars are scored separately for Adult Occupant Protection (AOP, out of 32) and Child Occupant Protection (COP, out of 49), using instrumented adult and child dummies to measure injury readings during each impact. A separate Safety Assist Technologies check confirms whether features like Electronic Stability Control and ISOFIX child-seat anchors are actually fitted, not just claimed. Bharat NCAP has run since October 2023 under standard AIS-197, and a car earns its star rating by clearing minimum point thresholds on both AOP and COP, not just an average.

Bharat NCAP vs Global NCAP: Don't Compare the Numbers Directly

Bharat NCAP scores Adult Occupant Protection out of 32. Global NCAP's updated protocol, used since 2023, scores AOP out of 34. The two programmes are broadly aligned in testing philosophy, AIS-197 explicitly references Global NCAP's protocols, but they're not on identical scales, so a car's Bharat NCAP score and its Global NCAP score (if it has one from an earlier or export-market test) shouldn't be read as directly comparable numbers. If you see conflicting star ratings for the same car cited from different sources, this is usually why.

Standard Safety Kit Across the Range

Six airbags, ABS with EBD, Electronic Stability Control, hill-hold assist, and ISOFIX child-seat anchors are standard across every Brezza trim, not gated to the top of the range. Higher trims from the ZXi upward add Level 1 ADAS functions, blind-spot warning, rear cross-traffic alert, and a tyre-pressure monitoring system, which sit above the baseline safety kit rather than replacing it. This matters because a 5-star crash-test result only tells part of the story; the fitted safety technology on top of it is what actually helps you avoid the crash in the first place. Full trim-by-trim breakdown of what's standard where is in our Variants Explained guide.

Worth Knowing: Bharat NCAP 2.0 Is Coming

A revised Bharat NCAP 2.0 protocol, expected around October 2027, will move to a 100-point, five-pillar system covering accident avoidance, vulnerable road user protection, and post-crash safety alongside crash performance, with heavier weighting toward ADAS. The current 5-star Brezza rating is under the existing AIS-197 protocol; it isn't retroactively affected by the new system, but expect star ratings across the industry to get harder to earn once 2.0 takes effect.

Our Take

The Brezza's 5-star Bharat NCAP rating is a genuine, well-clear-of-the-threshold result, not a marginal pass, and the safety kit backing it up is standard across the range rather than reserved for flagship trims. That's a meaningfully different story from the outgoing Brezza, which never held a comparable current-protocol rating. FAQs

What is the Brezza's Bharat NCAP rating?
5 stars, with a published Adult Occupant Protection score of 30.41 out of 32 and a Child Occupant Protection score of 43 out of 49.

What does a Bharat NCAP star rating actually measure?
Three crash tests (a 64km/h frontal offset test, a 50km/h side impact test, and a 29km/h side pole impact test), scored separately for Adult Occupant Protection (out of 32) and Child Occupant Protection (out of 49), plus a check for fitted Safety Assist Technologies like ESC and ISOFIX.

Is the Brezza's 5-star rating the same across all variants?
Bharat NCAP tests a specific variant, typically the base or a nominated trim, and the published rating applies to that tested configuration. Standard safety kit including 6 airbags, ESP, and ISOFIX is confirmed across the range.

How does Bharat NCAP differ from Global NCAP?
Bharat NCAP scores Adult Occupant Protection out of 32; Global NCAP's updated protocol scores it out of 34. The two programmes are broadly aligned in methodology but not directly interchangeable, so a car's Bharat NCAP and Global NCAP scores shouldn't be compared as if on the same scale.
