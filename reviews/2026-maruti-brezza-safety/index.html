Maruti Brezza Safety Rating: 5-Star Bharat NCAP (30.41/32) Explained

AI illustration: FlamingPiston
Gagan Mishra
Published Sep 3, 2026 · Updated Nov 23, 2026
4 min read
Category: Compact SUVs
Share

The 2026 Maruti Brezza has a five-star Bharat NCAP rating, with 30.41 out of 32 for adult occupant protection and 43 out of 49 for child occupant protection. The rating applies to the petrol and turbo-petrol versions; the CNG versions are not covered. Six airbags and ESP are standard on every Brezza.

At a glance

  • Rating: five stars, from July 2026.
  • Covers: 1.5 petrol manual, 1.5 automatic and 1.0 turbo manual. Not CNG.
  • Standard on all: six airbags, ESP, hill-hold assist, ISOFIX.
  • Not offered: ADAS, on any version.

The Brezza’s Bharat NCAP scores

TestScore
Adult occupant protection30.41 / 32
Frontal offset test14.41 / 16
Side barrier test16.00 / 16
Child occupant protection43.00 / 49
OverallFive stars (adult and child)

Bharat NCAP tested a ZXi+ with the 1.5 petrol engine and automatic gearbox, weighing 1,471 kg. The side barrier result was a maximum 16 out of 16. For comparison, the Tata Nexon’s rating (October 2024) is 29.41/32 for adults and 43.83/49 for children; see Brezza vs Nexon.

Which versions the rating covers

Bharat NCAP marks the Brezza’s rating as applying to select variants. Its list covers the petrol family: the 1.5 six-speed manual, the 1.5 six-speed automatic and the 1.0 turbo six-speed manual, across the trims they are sold in. The CNG variants are not listed. That does not mean the CNG car is unsafe: it has the same airbags and stability control. It means it has not been rated, so the five stars do not officially apply to it. Our Brezza CNG guide covers this in more detail.

Equipment from Maruti Suzuki’s New Brezza brochure. The sensor-based blind-spot and cross-traffic alerts are warnings, not driver assistance: the Brezza does not brake or steer for you. If you want the extra safety kit with an automatic, only the ZXi+ AT has it. See our Brezza variants guide for the full list.

Our take

The Brezza’s five-star result, with a perfect side-impact score, is a strong one, and the core safety kit is standard rather than kept for top trims. Two things to keep in mind: the rating does not cover the CNG, and there is no ADAS at any price. Autocar India also notes the Brezza no longer comes with a spare tyre, which matters on long trips. Read our Brezza review for the full picture.

What Owners Say

The facelifted Brezza went on sale in July 2026, so owner reports are still early and most cover under 2,000 km. On 23 November 2026 we read owner posts on Team-BHP, CarWale and CarDekho, leaving out test drives, posts about the older model, duplicate reviews and anyone who had not bought or driven the car. These are owners’ experiences, not measured results; we say how many owners each point rests on.

  • The rating matters to buyers. Two CarWale owners name the five-star Bharat NCAP rating as a reason they are happy with the car. Owner reviews can’t tell you how a car performs in a crash, so we rely on Bharat NCAP for that.
  • Brakes. One Team-BHP owner of a ZXi Turbo, at about 1,200 km, finds the brakes spongy and the stopping power only adequate. This is one owner’s view.
  • The spare wheel. One owner on each of Team-BHP and CarWale names the missing spare wheel as the main drawback.

Owner reports don’t change the safety picture, which comes from Bharat NCAP’s test. They do echo our caution about the spare wheel.

FAQs

What is the Brezza’s Bharat NCAP rating?
Five stars, with 30.41 out of 32 for adult occupant protection and 43 out of 49 for child occupant protection. The rating applies from July 2026.

Does the five-star rating cover the Brezza CNG?
No. Bharat NCAP lists the rating for the 1.5 petrol manual, 1.5 automatic and 1.0 turbo manual. The CNG variants are not on its list, so they have not been rated.

Which Brezza variant did Bharat NCAP test?
A ZXi+ with the 1.5 petrol engine and automatic gearbox, weighing 1,471 kg.

How many airbags does the Brezza have?
Six, on every variant, along with ESP, hill-hold assist, ISOFIX mounts and three-point seatbelts for all occupants.

Does the Brezza have ADAS?
No. The ZXi Turbo and ZXi+ have blind-spot warning, rear cross-traffic alert, safe-exit warning and front parking sensors, and the ZXi+ adds a 360-degree camera, but there is no ADAS on any version.
