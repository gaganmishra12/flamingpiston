2026 Maruti Brezza Automatic: Price, Mileage and Is the AT Worth It?

AI illustration: FlamingPiston
Gagan Mishra
Published Aug 17, 2026 · Updated Nov 23, 2026
5 min read
Category: Compact SUVs
Share

The Brezza’s automatic is a six-speed torque converter, not an AMT, and it comes only with the 1.5-litre petrol engine. It is offered on the VXi, ZXi and ZXi+, from ₹10.61 lakh to ₹13.76 lakh ex-showroom (23 November 2026). There is no automatic turbo, no automatic LXi and no automatic CNG.

Quick verdict

If most of your driving is in city traffic, the 1.5 automatic is the easiest Brezza to live with, and the ZXi AT is the one to buy. The cost is about ₹1.3 lakh over the equivalent manual, a slightly lower claimed mileage, and no option of the livelier turbo engine.

What you get

ItemBrezza automatic
Engine1.5-litre K15C petrol, mild hybrid
Output103 PS, 139 Nm
GearboxSix-speed torque-converter automatic
Claimed mileage20.17 km/l
TrimsVXi, ZXi, ZXi+

The 1.0 turbo-petrol makes more torque (170 Nm) but comes only with a manual. If you want a turbo engine with a torque-converter automatic from Maruti, the Fronx offers that pairing; see Brezza vs Fronx.

Prices as listed by CarDekho on 23 November 2026. The automatic costs ₹1.30 lakh more than the 1.5 manual in the same trim. Against the 1.0 turbo manual, the gap is bigger: ₹1.91 lakh on the VXi and ZXi, and ₹2.29 lakh on the ZXi+.

Which automatic to buy

ZXi AT (₹11.90 lakh) is the best balance. Over the VXi AT it adds an electric sunroof, LED projector headlamps, a reverse camera, wireless charging, push-button start and a 60:40 split rear seat.

VXi AT (₹10.61 lakh) is the cheapest way into a Brezza automatic, with a 7-inch touchscreen, wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay and auto climate control, but steel wheels with covers and no rear camera.

ZXi+ AT (₹13.60 lakh) adds the 10.1-inch screen, head-up display, 360-degree camera, ventilated front seats, cruise control, blind-spot warning and rear cross-traffic alert. On the ZXi, the last three come only with the turbo manual, so the ZXi+ is the only automatic that has them.

The full feature split is in our Brezza variants guide.

Mileage and driving

Maruti claims 20.17 km/l for the automatic, against 20.81–21.09 km/l for the 1.5 manual. As of 23 November 2026 we have not found an independent real-world test of the 2026 Brezza automatic; our Brezza mileage guide has the tests that exist, all of the turbo.

Autocar India describes the 1.5 as the engine that “remains the only way to have an automatic gearbox” in the Brezza, and rates the car’s cabin and space highly. Its criticisms of the car as a whole apply here too: it is not exciting to drive, the steering feels disconnected and the body feels top-heavy in corners. We have not driven the automatic ourselves.

Who should buy it

  • Buy it if you drive mostly in city traffic and want a conventional automatic rather than an AMT.
  • Skip it if you want CNG, want the turbo engine, or are on a budget below ₹10.6 lakh.

What Owners Say

The facelifted Brezza went on sale in July 2026, so owner reports are still early and most cover under 2,000 km. On 23 November 2026 we read owner posts on Team-BHP, CarWale and CarDekho, leaving out test drives, posts about the older model, duplicate reviews and anyone who had not bought or driven the car. These are owners’ experiences, not measured results; we say how many owners each point rests on.

  • No detailed automatic reports yet. We found no owner who has posted a driving report on the 2026 Brezza automatic. One Team-BHP member had only just taken delivery of a VXi AT when they posted.
  • The 1.5 engine itself. Owners of the 1.5 manual, which uses the same engine, describe it as smooth: a CarWale owner of a ZXi 1.5 manual calls it smooth and silent, and a CarDekho owner of the 1.5 finds the mileage good even in the city. That is two owners, and neither drives the automatic.

Until automatic owners report back, our advice rests on the specifications and Autocar India’s review. We will add owner reports here when they appear.

FAQs

Is the Brezza automatic a CVT or an AMT?
Neither. It is a six-speed torque-converter automatic, paired only with the 1.5-litre petrol engine.

What is the price of the Brezza automatic?
₹10.61 lakh for the VXi AT, ₹11.90 lakh for the ZXi AT and ₹13.60 lakh for the ZXi+ AT, ex-showroom in November 2026. Dual-tone paint adds about ₹16,000 on the ZXi and ZXi+.

Can I get the Brezza turbo with an automatic?
No. The 1.0 turbo-petrol is manual-only. The automatic comes only with the 1.5 petrol, and not on the base LXi or with CNG.

What is the mileage of the Brezza automatic?
Maruti claims 20.17 km/l, against 20.81 to 21.09 km/l for the 1.5 manual. We have not found an independent real-world test of the 2026 automatic yet.

How much more does the automatic cost than the manual?
About ₹1.3 lakh over the equivalent 1.5 manual (₹10.61 lakh vs ₹9.31 lakh for the VXi; ₹11.90 lakh vs ₹10.60 lakh for the ZXi). Against the turbo manual in the same trim, the automatic costs ₹1.91 lakh more on the VXi and ZXi.
