Mahindra BE 6 SPORTEQ: BaaS or Outright Purchase?

AI illustration: FlamingPiston
Gagan Mishra
Published Oct 23, 2026 · Updated Nov 21, 2026
8 min read Category: Electric
Share f 𝕏

Mahindra’s August 2026 update turned the BE 6 into the BE 6 SPORTEQ. It added a 70 kWh battery, a three-screen dashboard and Battery-as-a-Service, which cuts the entry price to ₹11.45 lakh. That headline price is a different purchase from owning the battery, so this review compares the two properly.

A Note on This Review

This review uses Mahindra’s published prices and announcement, the Bharat NCAP report, current listings and Autocar India’s reporting, including its October 2025 road test of the pre-SPORTEQ BE 6, not our own test. Prices and specifications were checked on 21 November 2026. See our Review Methodology for how we separate editorial assessment from measured testing.

Quick Verdict

The BE 6 is one of the quickest, best-handling and best-equipped EVs at its price, with a five-star crash rating. The rear seat is its weak point, with tight headroom and limited visibility. BaaS halves the entry price, but at ₹3.75 per km it suits low-mileage drivers best. Our pick is the Three with the 70 kWh battery, bought outright.

Mahindra’s prices exclude the wall charger and installation; listings show a second price about ₹75,000 higher for each trim, so confirm what your quote includes. Battery-as-a-Service is offered on the One and Two only, at ₹11.45 lakh and ₹12.95 lakh plus ₹3.75 per kilometre for the battery. At that rate, 15,000 km a year adds ₹56,250 in battery fees before electricity. Mahindra’s own example assumes 60 km a day. Read the agreement for minimum billing and exit terms before you sign.

2

Exterior Design

The BE 6’s angular, coupe-like shape is a big part of why people buy it, and the SPORTEQ update keeps it intact while adding new colours and the more aggressive Formula E (FE) versions. The design has consequences, though. The falling roofline and high tail restrict rear visibility and rear headroom, which we come to below. Under the bonnet there is a 45-litre front storage compartment.

Image: Mahindra & Mahindra
3

Interior Design & Practicality

From the Two upwards, the SPORTEQ gets a new three-screen dashboard that stretches across the cabin, including a screen for the front passenger. The base One and the FE versions keep two screens. Autocar India found the front seats comfortable. In the back, the tapering roofline makes headroom tight for tall passengers, under-thigh support is short and rear visibility is limited. That matters if you regularly carry adults in the back.

Image: Mahindra & Mahindra
4

Features & Safety

Even the One is well equipped. Autocar India lists six airbags, stability control, tyre-pressure monitoring, disc brakes all round, two 12.3-inch screens with wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay, automatic climate control, rear vents and over-the-air updates. The Two adds a 16-speaker Harman Kardon system with Dolby Atmos, a digital key, the passenger screen, a 540-degree camera that adds an underbody view, dual-zone climate control and wireless charging. The Three adds a panoramic glass roof, a powered driver’s seat with memory and ventilated front seats.

Image: Mahindra & Mahindra

Mileage, Engine & Performance

Every BE 6 SPORTEQ has a single rear-mounted motor. Power rises with the battery.

Published claimed ranges differ: Autocar India’s SPORTEQ guide gives 548, 651 and 683 km, while CarWale and CarDekho list 573, 651 and 708 km. Mahindra’s announcement does not state them, so we show both. DC fast charging from 20 to 80% takes about 20 minutes on a suitable charger. In its 2025 road test, Autocar India’s 79 kWh BE 6 reached 100 km/h in 7.14 seconds. Mahindra claims 6.44 seconds for the FE with its Acceleration Boost.

6

Tech & Safety Deep-Dive

This section draws on the published Bharat NCAP report and Autocar India’s reporting, not our own, in line with our Review Methodology. Bharat NCAP gave the BE 6 five stars from January 2025, with 31.97 out of 32 for adult protection and 45 out of 49 for child protection. The frontal offset test scored 15.97 out of 16 and the side barrier test a full 16. The test predates the SPORTEQ update, which kept the same body, so check the rating’s applicability for your exact variant.

On the road, Autocar India rated the BE 6’s ride and handling 9 out of 10. Its semi-active dampers make it feel tough over broken roads and composed through bends. The SPORTEQ One uses frequency-dependent dampers, and the FE versions get adaptive suspension.

Image: Mahindra & Mahindra

Why These Scores

Design: A striking shape that is a real reason to buy it.

Performance: Strong rear-motor performance from every battery.

Mileage Potential: About 450 km real-world from the 79 kWh pack in Autocar India’s hot-weather test.

Comfort: A well-judged ride, but a cramped rear seat for tall passengers.

Features: A very long equipment list, with the three-screen cockpit from the Two.

Safety: Five stars for the BE 6, though from a test before the SPORTEQ update.

Practicality: A 45-litre frunk helps, but rear space and visibility are limited.

Value for Money: BaaS lowers the entry price; outright prices are fair for the performance.

7

Verdict

The BE 6 SPORTEQ is a genuinely exciting electric SUV with the performance, ride and equipment to back up its looks. It is less convincing as a family car if tall adults often sit in the back. On the money question, BaaS suits buyers who drive little and want a low upfront price. If you drive a lot, buying outright is likely to cost less over time. Our pick is the Three 70 kWh, which balances range, equipment and price.

Image: Mahindra & Mahindra

Who Should Buy This

  • Keen drivers who want a fast, well-handling EV at this price
  • Tech-focused buyers who will use the three-screen cockpit and features
  • Low-mileage drivers attracted by the BaaS entry price
  • Buyers with reliable home or office charging

Who Should Avoid This

  • Families who regularly seat tall adults in the back
  • High-mileage drivers considering BaaS
  • Anyone who needs good rear visibility for daily driving

FAQs

What is the price of the Mahindra BE 6 SPORTEQ?
Mahindra lists it from ₹19.45 lakh to ₹26.95 lakh, ex-showroom, without the wall charger and installation. With Battery-as-a-Service, the One starts at ₹11.45 lakh.

How does the BE 6 BaaS plan work?
You buy the car without the battery for ₹11.45 lakh (One) or ₹12.95 lakh (Two) and pay ₹3.75 per kilometre for the battery.

What is the BE 6 SPORTEQ’s range?
Claimed figures differ between sources: about 548–573 km for 59 kWh, 651 km for 70 kWh and 683–708 km for 79 kWh. Autocar India’s 2025 test of the 79 kWh BE 6 returned 449 km combined.

What is the BE 6 Bharat NCAP rating?
Five stars from January 2025, with 31.97 out of 32 for adults and 45 out of 49 for children. The test predates the SPORTEQ update.

Which BE 6 SPORTEQ variant should I buy?
We suggest the Three with the 70 kWh battery for its balance of range, features and price.

Is the BE 6 rear seat comfortable?
Autocar India found rear headroom tight for tall passengers and under-thigh support short.
