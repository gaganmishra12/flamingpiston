2026 Maruti Brezza Variants Explained: Prices, Features and Which to Buy

Image: Maruti Suzuki, edited by FlamingPiston
Gagan Mishra
Published Aug 13, 2026 · Updated Nov 23, 2026
7 min read
Category: Compact SUVs
Share

The Brezza comes in four trims, LXi, VXi, ZXi and ZXi+, priced from ₹7.55 lakh to ₹13.76 lakh ex-showroom (23 November 2026). The trim is only half the decision: each one comes with a different set of engines and gearboxes, and some combinations are not offered at all. Start with the engine and gearbox you want, then pick the trim.

Three rules that narrow the choice

  • Turbo = manual. The 1.0 turbo-petrol comes only with a six-speed manual, on every trim.
  • Automatic = 1.5 petrol, VXi or higher. There is no automatic LXi and no automatic turbo.
  • CNG = LXi, VXi or ZXi, manual only. There is no CNG ZXi+.

Ex-showroom prices as listed by CarDekho on 23 November 2026, after Maruti’s price rise of up to ₹5,000 that day. Dual-tone paint, available on the ZXi and ZXi+, adds ₹10,000 to ₹16,000. That gives 13 engine, gearbox and trim combinations, or 19 listed variants once dual-tone versions are counted.

In every trim, the turbo is the cheapest engine: ₹61,000 to ₹85,000 less than the 1.5 manual. Autocar India points out that a 1.5 automatic costs over ₹2 lakh more than the equivalent turbo manual. Engine outputs are 110 PS and 170 Nm for the turbo, 103 PS and 139 Nm for the 1.5, and 87.8 PS and 121.5 Nm for the CNG on gas.

LXi: the safety basics, little else

From ₹7.55 lakh. Every Brezza, including the LXi, has six airbags, ESP, hill-hold assist, ISOFIX mounts, three-point seatbelts for all seats, rear AC vents, keyless entry and power windows all round. The LXi has no touchscreen or speakers, halogen projector headlamps, steel wheels with covers and no rear camera. The LXi Turbo adds auto climate control, a height-adjustable driver’s seat and a rear defogger. It is the trim for buyers who want the safety kit and the cheapest way into the turbo, and can add their own audio.

VXi: the cabin starts to feel complete

From ₹8.70 lakh (turbo). The VXi adds a 7-inch touchscreen with wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay, Arkamys speakers, steering-mounted controls, auto climate control, a height-adjustable driver’s seat and seat-back pockets. The VXi Turbo also gets painted alloy wheels, a reverse camera and electrically folding mirrors; the VXi 1.5 keeps wheel covers. It is the cheapest way to get an automatic (₹10.61 lakh) and the middle CNG option (₹9.99 lakh).

ZXi: the value pick

From ₹9.99 lakh (turbo). The ZXi adds an electric sunroof, dual-LED projector headlamps with floating LED DRLs, a reverse camera, wireless charging, push-button start, a PM2.5 cabin filter, a 60:40 split rear seat, a rear armrest, a tilt-and-telescopic steering column, a colour driver display and a luggage lamp. With the turbo, it also gets cruise control, front parking sensors, blind-spot warning, rear cross-traffic alert, safe-exit warning, tyre-pressure monitoring, auto headlamps and a rear wiper. Autocar India calls the ZXi Turbo the best-balanced turbo variant in the range.

The ZXi is also the top CNG version (₹11.65 lakh) and the cheaper of the two automatic trims above the VXi (₹11.90 lakh).

ZXi+: the full kit, but turbo or automatic only

From ₹11.31 lakh (turbo); the automatic is ₹13.60 lakh. Over the ZXi, the ZXi+ adds:

  • a 10.1-inch touchscreen with Suzuki Connect connected-car services and over-the-air updates
  • a head-up display and a 360-degree camera
  • ventilated front seats, leatherette upholstery and a leather-wrapped steering wheel
  • LED fog lamps and machined alloy wheels
  • 64-colour ambient lighting, an auto-dimming mirror and rear USB charging

The blind-spot warning, rear cross-traffic alert and cruise control are standard here with either engine. What it does not have: ADAS of any kind (no Brezza does), a digital instrument cluster, powered seats, a 1.5 manual or CNG.

Our pick

  • Best value: ZXi Turbo (₹9.99 lakh), for the sunroof, LED lights and the turbo-only safety kit.
  • Tightest budget: LXi Turbo (₹7.55 lakh) if you can live without a screen; otherwise VXi Turbo (₹8.70 lakh).
  • Automatic: ZXi AT (₹11.90 lakh). The VXi AT is ₹1.29 lakh cheaper but has no sunroof, LED lamps or split rear seat.
  • CNG: VXi CNG (₹9.99 lakh) for value; ZXi CNG (₹11.65 lakh) if you want the sunroof and split rear seat.
  • Everything: ZXi+ AT (₹13.60 lakh), the only way to get the 360-degree camera, head-up display and ventilated seats with an automatic.

Prices for the whole range are in our Brezza review. If you are deciding between engines, see Brezza turbo vs 1.5 petrol, or compare the Brezza with the Tata Nexon.

What Owners Say

The facelifted Brezza went on sale in July 2026, so owner reports are still early and most cover under 2,000 km. On 23 November 2026 we read owner posts on Team-BHP, CarWale and CarDekho, leaving out test drives, posts about the older model, duplicate reviews and anyone who had not bought or driven the car. These are owners’ experiences, not measured results; we say how many owners each point rests on.

  • The turbo trims feel like good value. Owners of the VXi Turbo, ZXi Turbo and ZXi+ Turbo on Team-BHP and CarWale (four in all) are happy with what they got for the price; a ZXi Turbo owner on Team-BHP calls its equipment hard to beat at about ₹11.2 lakh on the road.
  • The base car is bare. A CarWale owner of the LXi Turbo misses the touchscreen, alloy wheels and rear camera, and a CarDekho turbo owner says the head unit and speakers had to be added after purchase.
  • ZXi+ features get used. Two CarWale ZXi+ owners single out the 360-degree camera, ventilated seats and front and rear sensors as genuinely useful.
  • CNG trims feel short on kit. Three CarWale CNG owners say the CNG versions miss features, naming the fog lamps and 360-degree camera (ZXi+ only) and cruise control (turbo trims only).

Owners back our pick of the ZXi Turbo as the best value, and our warning that the LXi has no screen. If you want CNG, check the feature list carefully: owners notice what is missing.

FAQs

Which Brezza variant is the best value?
For most buyers, the ZXi. It adds a sunroof, LED projector headlamps, wireless charging, push-button start, a PM2.5 filter and a 60:40 split rear seat over the VXi. With the turbo engine, it also gets cruise control, front parking sensors, blind-spot warning and rear cross-traffic alert, from ₹9.99 lakh.

Can I get the Brezza turbo with an automatic gearbox?
No. The 1.0 turbo-petrol comes only with a six-speed manual, on all four trims. The six-speed torque-converter automatic is offered only with the 1.5 petrol, on the VXi, ZXi and ZXi+.

Does the Brezza have ADAS?
No. No Brezza variant has ADAS. The ZXi Turbo and ZXi+ have sensor-based blind-spot warning and rear cross-traffic alert, and the ZXi+ adds a 360-degree camera.

Can I get CNG on the top ZXi+ variant?
No. CNG is offered on the LXi, VXi and ZXi only, with a manual gearbox, from ₹9.45 lakh to ₹11.81 lakh.

What does the ZXi+ add over the ZXi?
A 10.1-inch touchscreen with connected-car features, a head-up display, a 360-degree camera, ventilated front seats, LED fog lamps, machined alloy wheels, leatherette seats and 64-colour ambient lighting. The ZXi+ starts at ₹11.31 lakh with the turbo.

Features by trim are taken from Maruti Suzuki’s New Brezza brochure; prices from CarDekho’s variant list on 23 November 2026. Check the exact variant at your dealer, as equipment can change.
