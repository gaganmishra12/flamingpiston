Maruti Swift 2026: Mileage, AMT or CNG? Which to Buy

AI illustration: FlamingPiston
Gagan Mishra
Published Oct 23, 2026 · Updated Nov 21, 2026
7 min read Category: Hatchback
Share f 𝕏

The fourth-generation Swift is now offered as a petrol manual, petrol AMT, CNG manual and, since September, a CNG AMT. All four share one three-cylinder engine, so the choice comes down to how you drive and how far.

A Note on This Review

This review uses Maruti Suzuki’s published specifications, current listed prices and other publications’ road tests, not our own test. Prices and specifications were checked on 21 November 2026. See our Review Methodology for how we separate editorial assessment from measured testing.

Quick Verdict

The Swift is an efficient, easy-to-drive small car with six airbags as standard. The petrol AMT suits traffic-heavy commutes, and CNG makes sense only if you drive a lot and can refuel easily. It has no Indian crash-test rating yet, and the rear and boot suit a couple or small family better than five adults.

2

Exterior Design

The Swift still looks like a Swift: a short, rounded hatchback with its wheels pushed out to the corners. The fourth generation adds a gloss-black grille, projector headlamps with boomerang-shaped running lights and C-shaped tail-lamps. It is 3,860 mm long, 1,735 mm wide and 1,520 mm tall, with a 2,450 mm wheelbase and 163 mm of ground clearance. That makes it easy to park and nimble in town.

Image: Maruti Suzuki India
3

Interior Design & Practicality

Autocar India found the front seats sporty and supportive, though snug for larger drivers, and the driving position excellent. The rear bench has room for two six-footers one behind the other. The middle passenger has no headrest, and the rear AC console gets in the way of their feet.

The boot is 265 litres, adequate for the class, with a 60:40 split rear seat. Autocar India noted a high loading lip. In CNG versions the gas cylinder sits at the back of the car, so check the boot of a CNG Swift before you buy one.

Image: Maruti Suzuki India
4

Features & Safety

Six airbags, electronic stability control and hill-hold assist are standard on every Swift, including the base LXi. That is the most important equipment fact about this car.

The VXi adds a touchscreen, and the ZXi gets alloy wheels. The higher trims bring automatic climate control, rear AC vents, a reversing camera, cruise control and wireless charging. Autocar India found the reversing camera’s picture less sharp than rivals’, without adaptive guidelines. Prioritise the AMT and rear vents over cosmetic upgrades.

Image: Maruti Suzuki India
5

Mileage, Engine & Performance

Every Swift uses the 1.2-litre three-cylinder Z-series engine, in petrol or factory-fitted CNG form.

EnginePower / TorqueTransmission Options
1.2L Z-Series Petrol (3-cyl)60 kW (82 PS) / 112 Nm5-speed Manual, 5-speed AMT; also offered with factory-fitted S-CNG

Autocar India found the engine trembles at idle but smooths out on the move. Maruti has tuned it for drivability rather than speed: the manual took 13 seconds to reach 100 km/h, slower than the old car, and the AMT about three seconds longer. Rolling acceleration in the gears is as good as or better than earlier Swifts. The AMT shifts smoothly at a moderate pace.

6

Tech & Safety Deep-Dive

This section draws on published specifications and other publications’ road tests, not our own, in line with our Review Methodology. The current Swift does not appear on Bharat NCAP’s ratings list, which we checked on 21 November 2026, and we found no Global NCAP result for this generation. Results for export Swifts from other test programmes use different specifications and protocols, so we have not used them. The six standard airbags and stability control are a strong baseline, but there is no Indian crash-test score to judge the structure.

Autocar India found the ride firm but absorbent at low speed, with sharp bumps felt at highway speeds, and grip and body control impressive. It is a fun car to drive in town.

Image: Maruti Suzuki India

Why These Scores

Design: Recognisably a Swift, with sharper lights and a tidy, compact shape.

Performance: Drivable and responsive in gear, but slow from rest and trembly at idle.

Mileage Potential: High ARAI claims and strong real-world figures; CNG cuts running costs further.

Comfort: Supportive front seats; firm ride and a cramped middle rear seat.

Features: Well equipped on higher trims; the reversing camera is basic.

Safety: Six airbags and ESP standard, but no Indian crash-test rating yet.

Practicality: A 265-litre boot with a high lip, smaller still in CNG versions.

Value for Money: Low entry price, efficient running and a wide choice of gearboxes and fuels.

7

Verdict

The Swift does what it always has: it is easy to drive and cheap to run. Six standard airbags make even the base car a sensible purchase. It is less convincing as a family car for five, and the lack of an Indian crash-test rating means its safety case rests on equipment alone. Our pick is the VXi (O) or ZXi petrol AMT for city driving. Choose CNG only if you cover high distances and can refuel easily.

Image: Maruti Suzuki India

Who Should Buy This

  • City drivers who want a fuel-efficient, easy automatic
  • High-mileage buyers who can make CNG pay
  • Couples and small families who mostly use the front seats
  • First-time buyers who want six airbags on a budget

Who Should Avoid This

  • Buyers who want a published Indian crash-test rating
  • Families who regularly seat three in the back or carry a lot of luggage
  • Drivers who want strong acceleration

FAQs

What is the price of the Maruti Swift in 2026?
CarWale and CarDekho list the Swift from ₹5.84 lakh to ₹8.89 lakh, ex-showroom, in November 2026.

What mileage does the Swift give?
Maruti claims 24.8 km/l for the manual, 25.75 km/l for the AMT and 32.85 km/kg for CNG. Autocar India recorded 12.7–14 km/l in the city and about 19 km/l on the highway.

Does the Swift have six airbags?
Yes. Maruti Suzuki fits six airbags as standard on every variant.

What is the Swift’s safety rating?
The current Swift is not on Bharat NCAP’s ratings list as of 21 November 2026, and we found no Global NCAP result for this generation.

Is there a Swift CNG automatic?
Yes. Maruti added CNG with an AMT in September 2026.

How much boot space does the Swift have?
265 litres, with a 60:40 split rear seat. In CNG versions the gas cylinder takes up space at the back.
