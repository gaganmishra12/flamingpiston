2026 Maruti Brezza CNG: Price, Mileage, Boot Space and Is It Worth It?

AI illustration: FlamingPiston
Gagan Mishra
Published Aug 23, 2026 · Updated Nov 23, 2026
6 min read
Category: Compact SUVs
Share

The Brezza CNG costs ₹9.45 lakh to ₹11.81 lakh ex-showroom (23 November 2026) and has the highest claimed efficiency in the range, 26.90 km/kg. The 2026 update moved the CNG tank under the car, so the CNG Brezza now keeps the full 328-litre boot. There are two catches: no Brezza has a spare wheel any more, and the five-star Bharat NCAP rating does not cover the CNG versions.

Quick verdict

Buy the Brezza CNG if you drive a lot and have CNG stations on your routes: it is the cheapest Brezza to run, and you no longer give up boot space. The VXi CNG is the value pick. Look elsewhere if you need an automatic, a spare wheel, or a crash rating that covers the exact car you buy.

Prices and what each CNG trim gets

VariantEx-showroomOver the 1.5 petrol manual

Prices as listed by CarDekho on 23 November 2026. All CNG versions have six airbags, ESP, hill-hold assist and keyless entry.

  • LXi CNG: no touchscreen or speakers, steel wheels, manual climate control.
  • VXi CNG: adds a 7-inch touchscreen with wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay, steering-mounted controls and auto climate control.
  • ZXi CNG: adds an electric sunroof, LED projector headlamps, a reverse camera, alloy wheels, push-button start, wireless charging and a 60:40 split rear seat.

The turbo-only safety kit (blind-spot warning, rear cross-traffic alert, cruise control) and the ZXi+ features (360-degree camera, head-up display, ventilated seats) are not available with CNG. Full details are in our Brezza variants guide.

Autocar India explains that the CNG Brezza moved to an underfloor tank to free up luggage space, following the Victoris, and that this is why the spare tyre has been dropped from every Brezza. The Tata Nexon CNG, by contrast, keeps a space-saver spare under the car but has a slightly smaller 321-litre boot. Our Brezza boot space guide has the full comparison.

Mileage and running cost

Maruti claims 26.90 km/kg for the CNG, against 20.81–21.09 km/l for the 1.5 petrol manual. As of 23 November 2026 we have not found an independent real-world test of the 2026 Brezza CNG, so the figures below use the claim and are an illustration, not a test result.

VersionClaimedFuel cost per km
CNG26.90 km/kg₹3.23
1.5 petrol manual21.09 km/l₹4.84

This uses Delhi prices on 23 November 2026 as listed by V3Cars: CNG at ₹86.98 a kg and petrol at ₹102.12 a litre. On those numbers, CNG saves about ₹1.61 a km. The VXi CNG’s ₹68,000 premium over the VXi petrol would take roughly 42,000 km to recover; the ₹1.05 lakh premium on the LXi and ZXi, roughly 65,000 km. Real-world efficiency, local fuel prices and time spent running on petrol will change these numbers, so redo the sum with your own: cost per km = fuel price ÷ mileage. Our Brezza mileage guide covers every version.

Safety: the rating does not cover CNG

Every Brezza CNG has six airbags, ESP, hill-hold assist and ISOFIX mounts. But Bharat NCAP’s five-star rating (30.41/32 adult, 43/49 child) lists only the petrol and turbo-petrol versions as covered; the CNG variants are not on its list. The CNG car has not been rated, which is not the same as a poor rating. The same is true of the Nexon CNG. See our Brezza safety guide.

Who should buy it

  • Buy it if you cover high monthly distances, have CNG stations on your usual routes, and are happy with a manual gearbox.
  • Skip it if you want an automatic, the top-trim features, a spare wheel, or a crash rating that covers your exact variant.

What Owners Say

The facelifted Brezza went on sale in July 2026, so owner reports are still early and most cover under 2,000 km. On 23 November 2026 we read owner posts on Team-BHP, CarWale and CarDekho, leaving out test drives, posts about the older model, duplicate reviews and anyone who had not bought or driven the car. These are owners’ experiences, not measured results; we say how many owners each point rests on.

  • Comfortable, with good brakes and suspension. A CarWale owner of the ZXi CNG praises the drive comfort, control, brakes and suspension.
  • Mileage as promised, but no figures. One ZXi CNG owner on CarWale says the mileage is as claimed; another calls it good. No CNG owner we found has posted a measured km/kg figure.
  • Missing features. All three CarWale CNG owners we found feel the CNG versions are short on equipment. They name the fog lamps and 360-degree camera, which only the ZXi+ has, and cruise control, which comes only on turbo trims. One says the turbo versions get more features.

Owners broadly back the CNG Brezza as comfortable and economical, but every CNG owner we found wanted more features. All three are on CarWale, so treat this as an early view.

FAQs

What is the price of the Brezza CNG?
₹9.45 lakh for the LXi CNG, ₹9.99 lakh for the VXi CNG and ₹11.65 lakh for the ZXi CNG (₹11.81 lakh with dual-tone paint), ex-showroom in November 2026.

What is the mileage of the Brezza CNG?
Maruti claims 26.90 km/kg. We have not found an independent real-world test of the 2026 Brezza CNG yet.

Does the Brezza CNG lose boot space?
No. The CNG tank sits under the car, so the CNG Brezza keeps the full 328-litre boot. As a result, no Brezza has a spare wheel; you get a puncture repair kit instead.

Is the Brezza CNG covered by the five-star Bharat NCAP rating?
No. Bharat NCAP lists the rating for the petrol and turbo-petrol versions only. The CNG has the same six airbags and ESP as every Brezza, but it has not been rated.

Is there a Brezza CNG automatic?
No. The CNG comes only with a six-speed manual, on the LXi, VXi and ZXi. The ZXi+ is not offered with CNG.
