2026 Maruti Brezza CNG Review: Mileage, Performance & Ownership

Most factory CNG SUVs make you choose between running cost and boot space, the tank eats a big chunk of your luggage area. The 2026 Brezza CNG is one of the few in its segment that doesn't force that trade-off, and that's the detail worth understanding before anything else about it.

The Detail Most Reviews Bury

The Brezza CNG's tank is mounted underbody, not in the boot. That means you get the full 328-litre boot space of the petrol version, not the shrunken, oddly-shaped space most factory CNG SUVs leave you with. If boot space has previously ruled out CNG for you on other cars, the Brezza is a genuine exception worth reconsidering.

Power & Performance: The Honest Trade-off

The 1.5-litre CNG engine makes around 87bhp and 121.5Nm, a real step down from the petrol version's 102bhp and 137-139Nm. That's the genuine cost of running on gas: CNG combustion inherently produces less power from the same displacement, and the Brezza is no exception. It's paired with a 6-speed manual only, no automatic option exists on CNG. In practice, this means noticeably softer acceleration than either petrol engine, particularly on inclines and at highway overtaking speeds. If performance is a priority for you, CNG isn't the engine to pick, this is a running-cost play first, everything else second.

This is where CNG earns its keep. Independent running-cost breakdowns put Brezza CNG at roughly ₹2.50-3.60 per km against roughly ₹6.50-7 per km on petrol, at typical current fuel prices, a genuinely large gap, not a marginal one. The CNG variant carries an upfront premium of roughly ₹90,000-95,000 over the equivalent petrol trim, and most cost breakdowns put the break-even point at around 38,000-40,000km. For a driver covering 1,500km a month, that's a payback period of roughly two years, after which the fuel savings are pure upside for the rest of ownership.

Ownership: What Changes With CNG

CNG ownership does bring a few genuine differences worth knowing before you commit. Servicing costs a modest amount more, roughly ₹800-1,200 extra per visit for CNG-specific filter and valve checks, covered in full in our Maintenance Cost guide. You're also dependent on CNG station availability in your city; this matters far less in metros with dense filling infrastructure than in smaller towns where stations can be sparse. And CNG is offered on LXi, VXi, and ZXi only, not the flagship ZXi+, so if you want the top trim's adaptive cruise control and bigger touchscreen, you'll need to give up the CNG option entirely to get it.

Pick the CNG If

  • You cover high monthly distance and want the lowest running cost available
  • Boot space matters to you, this is one of the few CNG SUVs that doesn't compromise it
  • You live somewhere with reliable, accessible CNG filling infrastructure
  • Outright performance isn't your priority

Pick Petrol or Turbo Instead If

  • You drive lighter monthly distances, where the payback period stretches out
  • You want the range-topping ZXi+ trim, which isn't offered with CNG
  • Acceleration and outright response matter more than running cost to you
  • CNG availability is inconsistent or inconvenient where you live

Our Verdict

The Brezza CNG is a genuinely well-executed version of a powertrain that's usually a compromise elsewhere in the segment. The underbody tank solving the boot-space problem is the standout engineering decision here, and the running-cost math is compelling for anyone covering serious monthly distance. The trade-offs are real but honest: softer performance, no automatic, and no access to the flagship trim. If your priority is minimising cost per kilometre without giving up practicality, this is one of the more convincing CNG SUVs on sale right now. FAQs

Does the Brezza CNG lose boot space?
No. Unlike most factory CNG SUVs, the Brezza's CNG tank is mounted underbody rather than in the boot, so it retains the full 328-litre boot capacity of the petrol version.

What variants is the Brezza CNG available on?
LXi, VXi, and ZXi. It is not offered on the range-topping ZXi+, which is petrol-only.

What is the Brezza CNG's mileage and power?
Claimed mileage is up to 26.9 km/kg. The 1.5-litre CNG engine makes around 87bhp and 121.5Nm, noticeably less than the petrol version, since CNG combustion inherently produces less power from the same displacement.

Is the Brezza CNG worth it over petrol?
For high-mileage drivers, yes. Independent running-cost estimates put CNG at roughly ₹2.50-3.60 per km versus ₹6.50-7 on petrol, with the CNG price premium typically recovered within 20-40,000km depending on usage.
