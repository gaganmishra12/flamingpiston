Kia Seltos GTX(O), X-Line(O) variants launched at Rs 21.57 lakh
These new Seltos variants pack an expanded roster of safety equipment, including Level 2+ ADAS and rain-sensing wipers.
2 min read
•
1 Jul '26
Viraaj Bhatnagar
The
Kia Seltos
variant line-up has been expanded with two new trim levels: GTX(O) and X-Line(O). These pack additional safety kit and creature comforts over the previous top-spec trims of the Seltos, namely the GTX(A) and X-Line(A).
Kia Seltos GTX(O), X-Line(O) variant prices and details
Available only in turbo-petrol DCT and diesel AT guises
Both the
Seltos GTX(O)
and X-Line(O) can be had with only the automatic versions of the 160hp 1.5-litre turbo-petrol and the 116hp 1.5-litre diesel engines. The turbo-petrol is paired with a 7-speed dual-clutch automatic, while the diesel gets a 6-speed torque converter. Here's the full updated price list for the Kia Seltos:

The headliner of the Seltos GTX(O) and X-Line(O) trims is an expanded 'Level 2+ ADAS' suite. Compared to the
Seltos GTX(A)
and its 21-strong list of ADAS features, the GTX(O) adds seven more, for a total of 28. The new Seltos ADAS features comprise Junction Crossing, Lane Change Oncoming, Lane Change Side, and Evasive Steering Assist functions for the frontal collision avoidance system, as well as navigation-based adaptive cruise control for corners and speed-limited zones, and highway driving assist.
Alongside the broadened ADAS suite, the Seltos GTX(O) packs navigation functions for the 12.3-inch digital driver's display, one-touch auto up/down for all windows, a head-up display, built-in front and rear dashcams, rain-sensing wipers, and VR and navigation features in the Kia Connect mobile app.
Meanwhile, the
Seltos X-Line(O)
maintains feature parity with the GTX(O), but with cosmetic upgrades like an exclusive Matte Graphite exterior finish, a black-and-green interior theme, blacked-out exterior trim, and black 18-inch alloy wheels.
All prices are ex-showroom, India.
