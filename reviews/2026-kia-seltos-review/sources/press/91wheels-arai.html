New Kia Seltos Mileage Figures Out: Diesel Leads, Turbo Petrol Surprises

Arjun Sharma
•April 23, 2026

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

  • Diesel manual offers best efficiency at 19.4 km/l
  • Turbo petrol DCT returns higher mileage than iMT
  • NA petrol delivers 16.5 km/l across both gearboxes

The updated Kia Seltos, launched earlier this year, has now received its official ARAI-certified fuel efficiency figures. As one of the most popular SUVs in the mid-size segment, Kia Seltos mileage continues to play a key role in buyer decisions, and the latest numbers reveal a few interesting trends across engine options. With the second-generation model growing in size and gaining more features, it has also become slightly heavier. As a result, the fuel efficiency figures see a marginal dip compared to the outgoing version. That said, let's get into the details, but before that, make sure to join the 91wheels WhatsApp Community, specifically curated for petrolheads like us to keep up with the latest news and automotive updates.

Kia Seltos Mileage ARAI Figures Out: Up to 19.4 Kmpl

The 1.5-litre diesel engine of the Kia Seltos once again stands out as the most fuel-efficient option in the lineup. The manual version is rated at 19.4 km/l, while the automatic delivers 17.8 km/l. Producing 116 bhp and 250 Nm, this engine continues to be the go-to choice for buyers looking at long-distance efficiency and torque-rich performance.

Kia's 1.5-litre naturally aspirated petrol engine continues to account for a major share of sales. Available with both a manual and an IVT automatic gearbox, it returns an identical mileage figure of 16.5 km/l in both configurations. With outputs of 113 bhp and 144 Nm, it offers a balanced mix of performance and everyday usability.

Turbo Petrol Brings An Unexpected Twist

The 1.5-litre turbo petrol engine delivers the most surprising result in the lineup. While the iMT variant is rated at 15.6 km/l, the 7-speed DCT version is significantly more efficient at 17.5 km/l. This is unusual, as automated manuals are typically expected to be more fuel-efficient. With 158 bhp and 253 Nm on tap, the turbo variant also remains the most performance-focused option in the range.

The new-generation Seltos now sits on a new global platform with a longer wheelbase, translating into improved cabin space and road presence. Inside, the SUV features a large 30-inch Trinity display setup that integrates the infotainment system, digital instrument cluster, and climate controls. It also benefits from enhanced connected tech with OTA updates, along with a more refined overall design.

Also Read- Renault Triber To Get Turbo Petrol & Factory-Fitted Dual-Cylinder CNG

Globally, the Seltos is now sold in multiple markets, including the US and South Korea, and Kia has already showcased a hybrid version internationally. This is expected to arrive in India around 2027, adding another dimension to the lineup.

Verdict

The latest mileage figures of the Kia Seltos reflect a slight trade-off for its increased size and added features, but the overall package is now class leading. The diesel continues to be the efficiency champion, while the turbo petrol DCT stands out with its unexpected balance of performance and economy. With multiple engine options, strong feature upgrades, and a hybrid on the horizon, the Seltos continues to strengthen its position as one of the most versatile SUVs in its segment, essentially sitting at the top of its game with very few rivals currently able to match up to its status.
