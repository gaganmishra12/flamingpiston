    Four Variants of Kia Seltos Removed from the Line-Up

    Read inहिंदी
    Jay Shah
    November 20, 2026, 09:06 AM IST
    21,280 Views
    • iMT gearbox variants discontinued
    • Turbo-petrol variants now offered only with seven-speed DCT

    Kia has revised the Seltos line-up and discontinued four variants. The most significant change is that the 1.5-litre turbo-petrol’s clutchless manual (iMT) gearbox has been removed from the range. The discontinued variants are the HTE (O), HTK, and HTK (O) with the iMT, and the X-Line with the 1.5-litre naturally aspirated petrol with CVT. Kia has not made an official announcement yet, so buyers should check variant availability with their nearest dealer.

    With the iMT gone, the 158bhp, 253Nm turbo-petrol engine is now paired only with the seven-speed dual-clutch automatic. The cheapest turbo-petrol Seltos is now the HTK (O) DCT at Rs. 16.62 lakh, ex-showroom.

    When the new-generation Seltos launched, the iMT versions of the turbo-petrol started at Rs. 12.89 lakh with the HTE (O). So the entry point to the turbo-petrol has moved up by a fair margin, and buyers who wanted a manual turbo-petrol SUV no longer have one in the Seltos. The X-Line is the other casualty. The version with the naturally aspirated petrol and CVT has been dropped, which leaves the automatic X-Line variants in the range with the ADAS suite.

    The rest of the line-up carries on with the naturally aspirated petrol and the diesel, with the choice of manual, CVT, and automatic gearboxes depending on the variant. The Seltos is now priced from Rs 11.25 lakh to Rs. 21.82 lakh, ex-showroom, and competes with the Hyundai Creta, Skoda Kushaq, Tata Sierra, Toyota Urban Cruiser Hyryder, Maruti Suzuki Victoris, and Renault Duster.
