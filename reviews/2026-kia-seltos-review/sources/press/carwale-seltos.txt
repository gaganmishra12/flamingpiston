Why Buy Kia Seltos?
Highlights

Highlights
Walkthrough
The dual-12.3-inch infotainment displays are housed in a single panoramic panel.
New Seltos can be had with 16, 17, and 18-inch wheels and five alloy options (based on variant choice).
It gets 447-litres of boot space, which can be expanded by folding the rear seats.
The Kia Seltos 2026 gets six airbags as standard.
The new Seltos 2026 is 95mm longer (at 4460mm), 30mm wider (at 1830mm) and has an 80mm bigger wheelbase (at 2690mm).
The Seltos features an aggressive grille, along with unique DRLs and LED headlamps that give it a striking road presence.
The Star Map LED connected tail lamps and sporty spoiler define its futuristic identity, inspired by Kia’s EV lineup.
The dual-pane panoramic sunroof floods the cabin with light, enhancing the premium ambience and providing an expansive view for all occupants.
Tucked away for emergencies, the steel spare wheel either gets a 205/65 R16 or 215/60 R17 tyre, depending on the variant.
With a 447-litre capacity, the boot remains a strong point with adequate space for luggage, maintaining the Seltos' practical appeal.
The second row seats are comfortable for long journeys, has sunblinds, but the dark interior theme can limit the sense of airiness.
It features power adjustments with memory, upmarket mesh headrests, and also hard plastic seatbacks.
Part of the seamless panoramic display, the digital cluster includes a Blind View Monitor for safer, more tech-forward navigation.
Driving in tight spaces and difficult situations is effortless with a 360-degree camera and blind view monitor for driver awareness.
The refined cockpit layout places key controls at your fingertips, ensuring a driver-centric experience that feels premium and well-integrated.
The clean, premium cabin features a Trinity Panoramic Display and soft-touch materials; build quality feels solid and significantly improved.
Integrated within the clean dashboard design, the climate system provides intuitive comfort and includes dedicated rear vents.
Paired with a smooth gearboxes, the shifter manages traffic effortlessly, providing a stress-free driving experience.

How is the Kia Seltos car?
Pros
The interior quality, material finish, and overall cabin ambience feel genuinely premium.
The turbo-petrol engine delivers strong performance with smooth, refined power delivery.
The DCT automatic gearbox is quick-shifting, smooth in traffic, and easy to live with.
High-end features like a large panoramic sunroof, digital displays and sun-blinds enhance comfort.
Ride quality has improved over the previous model, with better bump absorption.
Cons
The diesel powertrain could use more high-speed grunt.
Despite improvements, the ride quality is still on the firmer side in the segment.
The turbo-petrol engine isn’t very fuel-efficient, especially in city driving conditions.
Some hard plastic elements feel out of place in an otherwise premium cabin.

The turbo-petrol engine feels powerful during overtakes and highway cruising, while the automatic transmission makes city driving stress-free. The cabin is spacious, comfortable, and packed with useful features for daily use. Ride quality is well balanced, and the steering feels confident at higher speeds. Safety features and build quality inspire confidence on long trips. Mileage is decent for a turbo petrol automatic when driven sensibly. Overall, the Seltos HTK(O) DCT offers a great blend of performance, comfort, practicality, and value, making it an excellent family SUV for both city and highway driving.
