The bold look of Badass.

Step into the all-new Seltos, where style meets attitude. With its aggressive, muscular build, striking shoulder lines, and fierce front and rear, it’s built to stand out.

Bigger space with increased dimensions​
With enhanced dimensions, best-in-segment length of 4,460 mm (+95 mm), higher ground clearance, and expanded interior space, the all-new Seltos is designed for your comfort and practicality on every drive.

Automatic Streamline Door Handles

Iconic R18- 46.20 cm (18”) Sporty Crystal Cut Alloy Wheels & Neon Brake callipers​

Striking Star Map LED connected Tail Lamps
Signature Star Map LED connected Tail Lamps light up the rear profile, adding a bold, futuristic touch that’s impossible to ignore.​

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

-

What is the price of the New Kia Seltos Base and Top Model?

The official ex-showroom price of the All-New Kia Seltos base model HTE starts at ₹ 11,24,900* and top model X Line (O) starts at ₹ 21,56,900*

Does new Kia Seltos have sunroof?

Yes, the new Kia Seltos comes with a Dual Pane Panoramic Sunroof, which offers passengers a sense of openness and luxury. The dual-pane panoramic sunroof is available on all Seltos variants starting from the HTK (O).

What are the seating options available in the Seltos?

The Kia Seltos is a 5-seater compact SUV that comes with front ventilated seats along with many other features.

How many airbags are there in New Kia Seltos?

The all-new Kia Seltos offers 6 airbags and a robust 24-standard-feature safety pack to ensure comprehensive protection for you and your loved ones.

What are the engine and transmission options available in the all-new Kia Seltos?

The all-new Kia Seltos offers three engine choices: a Smartstream G1.5 petrol engine with 6 speed manual/IVT transmission, a Smartstream G1.5 T-GDi petrol engine with 6iMT/ 7DCT, and 1.5l CRDi VGT Diesel engine with 6MT/ 6 speed automatic transmission.

How many features does the new Kia Seltos offer in its ADAS system?

The Kia Seltos comes with ADAS Level 2+ along with 21 autonomous safety features designed to keep you and your family secure through every mile.

Does new Seltos have cruise control?

Yes, new Kia seltos have Smart Cruise Control (SCC) with Stop & Go feature which automatically maintains distance and manages stop-and-go traffic

How many colour options are available for the all-new Kia Seltos?

The all-new Kia Seltos is available in 10 Monotone Colours: Morning Haze, Magma Red, Frost Blue, Gravity Gray, Imperial Blue, Glacier White Pearl, Pewter Olive, Ivory Silver Gloss, Aurora Black Pearl(Also available in X-Line) and Xclusive Matte Graphite (X-Line)

SUMMARY

  • The all-new Kia Seltos is backed by a 5-star BNCAP safety rating.
  • Its exterior includes Intense Ice Cube LED Projection Headlamps with Star Map LED DRLs, Iconic R18 Sporty Crystal Cut Alloy Wheels with Neon Callipers, Automatic Streamline Door Handles and Striking Star Map LED connected Tail Lamps.
  • A Dual Pane Panoramic Sunroof and Driver Seat Welcome Retract are also part of the package.
