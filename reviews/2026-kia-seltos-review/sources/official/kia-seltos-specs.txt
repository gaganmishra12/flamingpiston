FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
What are the dimensions of the all-new Kia Seltos?
The all-new Kia Seltos has an overall length of approximately 4,460 mm, a width of around 1,830 mm, and a height of roughly 1,635 mm.
What is new Seltos tyre size?
The New Seltos is available with tyre size of 205/65 R16 - 40.56 cm (16”), 215/60 R17 - 43.66 cm (17”), 215/55 R18 - 46.2 cm (18”)
What is new Kia Seltos Fuel tank capacity?
The new Kia Seltos comes with a 47-litre fuel tank capacity for the Smartstream G1.5 petrol engine and the 1.5L CRDi VGT diesel engine. It offers a 50-litre fuel tank capacity for the Smartstream G1.5 T-GDi petrol engine.
How much boot space does a new Kia Seltos have?
The new Kia Seltos Compact SUV car can be your perfect companion on a long drive with a boot space of 447** litres.
How many cc is a new Kia Seltos engine?
The new Kia Seltos is available with 1482 cc and 1497 cc petrol engines. In diesel, it comes with a 1493 cc engine.
SUMMARY
The all-new Kia Seltos measures 4460mm in overall length, 1830mm in overall width and 1635mm in overall height.
It is offered in trims HTE, HTE(O), HTK, HTK(O), HTX, HTX(A), GTX, X-Line, GTX(A) and X-Line (A), with exterior colours including Frost Blue, Pewter Olive, Ivory Silver Gloss, Imperial Blue, Aurora Black Pearl, Magma Red, Morning Haze, Glacier White Pearl and Gravity Gray.
Features listed include Star Map LED DRLs, Ice Cube MFR LED Headlamps, the Kia Digital Tiger Face Grille and a 26.04cm (10.25”) HD Touchscreen with Wireless Android Auto/Apple CarPlay.
