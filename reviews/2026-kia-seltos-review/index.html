Kia Seltos 2026: Which Engine and Variant to Buy

AI illustration: FlamingPiston
Gagan Mishra
Published Oct 23, 2026 · Updated Nov 21, 2026
8 min read Category: SUV
Share f 𝕏

Since its launch, the second-generation Seltos has gained a top GTX(O) trim, had a price rise and, this week, lost its turbo-petrol iMT. This review is updated for all three.

A Note on This Review

This review uses Kia India’s published specifications and prices, the Bharat NCAP report and other publications’ drives, not our own test. Prices and specifications were checked on 21 November 2026. See our Review Methodology for how we separate editorial assessment from measured testing.

Quick Verdict

The Seltos is one of the safest, most complete family SUVs you can buy at this price, and its best version is not the most expensive one. Pick the engine first, then stop at HTK(O) or HTX unless you specifically want the driver-assistance pack. The one thing to watch: with the iMT gone, the turbo-petrol now starts well into the middle of the range.

These are the starting prices for each trim. Kia sells most trims with more than one engine and gearbox, and the automatic and diesel versions cost more, so price the exact combination you want. The GTX and X-Line pairs cost the same: X-Line is a styling pack with darker trim and black wheels.

Two changes since launch matter here. Kia added the GTX(O) and X-Line(O) on 1 July 2026, with a fuller driver-assistance suite. And CarWale reported on 20 November 2026 that the turbo-petrol iMT versions have been dropped. Kia has not announced this, but its current brochure lists the turbo-petrol with the 7-speed DCT only. CarWale puts the cheapest turbo-petrol, the HTK(O) DCT, at ₹16.62 lakh ex-showroom.

2

Exterior Design

The new Seltos moves to Kia’s K3 platform and has grown to match. It is 4,460 mm long, 1,830 mm wide and has a 2,690 mm wheelbase. Autocar India notes that is 95 mm longer than before, with an 80 mm longer wheelbase, which makes it the longest car in its class. The extra size helps the cabin. It also means a bigger footprint in a city parking space.

The styling is loud. Tall LED daytime running lights wrap up over the bonnet, square headlamps sit in a wide gloss-black grille, and the rear has vertical tail-lamps joined by a light bar. Higher trims get 18-inch alloys, and the X-Line pack adds black wheels and darker trim. It is a design you will either like at first sight or not at all.

Image: Kia India
3

Interior Design & Practicality

The cabin is a strong point. Autocar India rated the fit and finish very good. It also liked that Kia kept physical toggles for temperature and fan speed, although the 5-inch climate screen sits behind the steering wheel rim. Rear-seat legroom is generous enough for six-footers, by the same account, with headroom that is only just adequate for them and a seat base that is short for tall passengers.

The boot holds a claimed 447 litres (VDA method), with a full-size spare under the floor. The Seltos is strictly a five-seater. If you need a third row, it is the wrong car.

Image: Kia India
4

Features & Safety

Every Seltos gets six airbags, electronic stability control, hill-start assist, downhill brake control, disc brakes on all four wheels, tyre-pressure monitoring, ISOFIX mounts and three-point belts with reminders for every seat. Kia calls this its 24-item standard safety pack.

The useful features arrive in steps. The HTK(O) adds the dual-pane panoramic sunroof, wireless charging and front parking sensors, plus ventilated front seats on automatics. The HTX brings the 12.3-inch touchscreen, Bose audio, dual-zone climate control and ventilated seats on every gearbox. The (A) trims add the Trinity screen panel, a 360-degree camera and Level 2 driver assistance with 21 functions. The GTX(O) goes to 28 functions and adds a head-up display, digital key, dashcam and air purifier. Rear ventilated seats are not offered on any trim.

Image: Kia India

Mileage, Engine & Performance

There are three 1.5-litre engines: a naturally aspirated petrol, a turbo-petrol and a diesel. They carry over from the first-generation car. Kia quotes outputs in kW; the PS figures below are our conversions.

1.5L Turbo-Petrol117.5 kW (~160 PS) / 253 Nm7-speed DCT
1.5L Diesel85 kW (~116 PS) / 250 Nm6-speed Manual, 6-speed Automatic

Autocar India found the turbo-petrol smooth and strong, though its dual-clutch gearbox can fumble at crawling speeds. CarWale called it one of the most competent powertrains in the segment. The diesel is refined but, in Autocar India’s view, lacks punch, and its automatic shifts slowly. With the iMT gone, the turbo-petrol is now automatic-only and starts higher up the range.

6

Tech & Safety Deep-Dive

This section draws on the published Bharat NCAP report and other publications’ drives, not our own, in line with our Review Methodology. The Seltos earned five stars from Bharat NCAP in March 2026: 31.70 out of 32 for adult protection and 45.00 out of 49 for child protection. The frontal offset test scored 15.70 out of 16 and the side barrier test a full 16, and the side pole test was rated OK. Bharat NCAP tested the HTE(O) and GTX diesel automatics. The rating is listed as a base rating that applies to the variants in its annexure. When the results came out, Kia said the adult score was the highest yet for a combustion-engined car in the programme.

The driver-assistance suite is standard from HTX(A) upward. Autocar India found the lane-departure correction natural but the lane-centring busy on unevenly marked roads. Treat it as an aid on good highways, not a reason to pay for a higher trim.

Why These Scores

Design: Strong presence, but the busy front end will divide opinion.

Performance: The turbo-petrol DCT is strong; published drives find the diesel short of punch.

Mileage Potential: ARAI claims are competitive, led by the diesel manual; no test by us.

Comfort: Published drives report a softer ride without losing body control.

Features: Broad equipment, with useful driver assistance from HTX(A), though rear ventilated seats are not offered.

Safety: Five stars from Bharat NCAP, with a record adult score for a combustion-engined car when published.

Practicality: Big cabin and a 447-litre boot, offset by a larger parking footprint.

Value for Money: The middle trims make sense; higher prices and a dearer turbo entry hurt the rest.

7

Verdict

The second-generation Seltos does not reinvent the formula, and it does not need to. It is bigger inside, rides more comfortably according to the people who have driven it, and backs up its safety claims with one of the best Bharat NCAP results so far. The risk is the trim ladder. Each step adds something attractive, and it is easy to spend ₹5 lakh more than you planned. Choose the engine first: petrol IVT for city driving, turbo-petrol DCT if you enjoy driving, diesel for long distances. Then make each step up beyond HTK(O) earn its place.

Image: Kia India

Who Should Buy This

  • Families who want a five-seat SUV with a top Bharat NCAP result
  • Buyers who want a choice of petrol, turbo-petrol and diesel
  • Long-distance drivers who still want a diesel automatic in this segment
  • Anyone who found the previous Seltos too firm over bad roads

Who Should Avoid This

  • Buyers who need seven seats
  • City drivers who need a smaller footprint for tight parking
  • Anyone who wanted an affordable turbo-petrol with a clutchless manual

FAQs

What is the price of the 2026 Kia Seltos?
Kia India lists trim-entry prices from ₹11,24,900 (HTE) to ₹21,56,900 (GTX(O) and X-Line(O)), ex-showroom. Diesel-automatic versions of the top trims cost more.

Is the Kia Seltos turbo-petrol still available with an iMT?
No. CarWale reported on 20 November 2026 that the iMT versions have been dropped, and Kia’s current brochure lists the turbo-petrol with the 7-speed DCT only. Kia has not made an announcement.

What is the Seltos Bharat NCAP rating?
Five stars for both adult and child protection, with 31.70 out of 32 and 45.00 out of 49, published in March 2026.

What mileage does the Seltos give?
ARAI-certified figures are 16.5 kmpl for the 1.5 petrol, 17.5 kmpl for the turbo-petrol DCT, 19.4 kmpl for the diesel manual and 17.8 kmpl for the diesel automatic. Expect less in daily driving.

Which Seltos variant has the panoramic sunroof?
The dual-pane panoramic sunroof starts from the HTK(O) trim.

Is the Kia Seltos a seven-seater?
No. It is a five-seater with a claimed 447-litre boot.
