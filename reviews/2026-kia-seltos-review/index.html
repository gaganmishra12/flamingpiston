Kia Seltos Review 2026: Engines, Features and Value

The Seltos offers enough combinations to make a showroom visit confusing. Petrol or diesel, manual or automatic, comfort equipment or a more expensive technology package: each choice can move the final bill. Its appeal is the breadth of the range. Its buying risk is spending more while losing sight of what you wanted the car to do.

Specification-based buying review, checked 23 October 2026. We have not road-tested this vehicle for this article. Quoted figures are manufacturer information; recommendations are our editorial assessment.

Price: Compare Powertrains Before Trim Names

Kia’s trim comparison page listed the range from ₹11,24,900 at our October check. It also showed substantially higher starting figures for the upper trim families. A trim-family price is not a complete quotation for every engine and gearbox available under that name.

Choose your transmission first, then compare equipment within that powertrain. We would start with the least expensive version that includes your non-negotiable comfort and parking features. Moving upwards only makes sense when you can name the benefit you will use.

Which Engine Makes Sense?

Kia lists a naturally aspirated 1.5 petrol with manual or IVT, a 1.5 turbo-petrol with iMT or DCT, and a 1.5 diesel with manual or automatic choices. Availability depends on trim. An iMT still requires the driver to select gears; it should not be confused with a fully automatic transmission.

For mainly urban use, the petrol IVT is our first comparison point. Include the turbo-petrol DCT if stronger acceleration is a priority, and evaluate its low-speed behaviour separately. For frequent longer journeys, compare the diesel’s total cost using your actual annual distance rather than assuming the fuel type always saves money.

Cabin: More Than the Screen Count

The official dimensions give you a useful starting point for checking your parking space. Measure the usable width between pillars and allow room to open the doors. A car fitting inside the painted rectangle does not mean everybody can climb out comfortably.

Inside, set the front seats normally before assessing rear legroom. Try the middle rear seat with the people who will use it, and load the bags you travel with. We have not measured the cabin or evaluated ride comfort on this model, so there is no invented verdict about how three adults feel on a long trip.

Features and Driver Assistance

Kia advertises a panoramic sunroof from HTK(O) and an available Level 2+ assistance package. Verify the exact feature list of the selected variant. The presence of ADAS in the range does not mean it comes with the starting-price car.

Prioritise equipment by use. A parking camera, comfortable seat adjustment and effective climate controls can make a difference on every journey. A sunroof may be enjoyable but does not resolve an uncomfortable rear seat. Driver assistance also leaves the driver responsible for observation and control.

The Ownership Test

Request a scheduled-service estimate, tyre replacement quote and insurance comparison for the version you want. A large discount is less impressive if accessories or a costly policy absorb it. Ask which connected features require a continuing subscription and what works without one.

The Seltos is a strong on-paper candidate for a buyer who wants a five-seat SUV with several powertrain options. Buyers who only need economical local transport should compare the final bill with a smaller car. Those who regularly need more than five seats should choose a different shortlist rather than compromise on seating requirements.

Flaming Piston's Take

The best Seltos is the one with the right engine, gearbox and everyday equipment for your household. Start with a petrol automatic for a city-led shortlist, and add diesel or turbo-petrol alternatives only when your usage supports them. Avoid selecting a top trim before you have settled those decisions.

FAQs

Is the Seltos a seven-seater?
No. Kia lists it as a five-seat SUV.

Does every Seltos have a panoramic sunroof?
No. Kia states availability from HTK(O); check the current variant sheet.

Which automatic should I compare first?
For mainly city use, start by assessing the petrol IVT, then compare other powertrains against your needs.
