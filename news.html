Mahindra Scorpio N Facelift Reaches Dealerships

The updated Scorpio N has begun arriving at dealerships across the country, with cosmetic changes led by new two-tone alloy wheels and revised cabin trim, while the mechanical package carries over unchanged. A full reveal is expected around Mahindra's Independence Day event.

News

Volvo EX90 Set to Land as Brand's New India Flagship

Volvo's fully-electric EX90 arrives this month as the brand's third EV in India after the XC40 and EX30. Built on the same platform as the outgoing XC90, it's expected in dual-motor and single-motor configurations with a claimed range beyond 550km on the larger battery.

News

Skoda Slavia Facelift Confirmed for August 18 Launch

Skoda is refreshing the Slavia for the first time since its 2022 debut, with an updated illuminated grille, revised LED lighting, and a larger digital driver's display. The existing 1.0-litre and 1.5-litre TSI petrol engines are expected to continue, alongside a new automatic gearbox option.
