Toyota Innova Hycross Facelift Teased Ahead of 25 November Launch: Should You Wait?

AI illustration: FlamingPiston
Gagan Mishra
Published Nov 18, 2026
3 min read
Category: News
Share

Update, 26 November 2026: Toyota launched the facelifted Innova Hycross on 25 November, priced from ₹19.59 lakh ex-showroom, with a flex-fuel strong hybrid from ₹19.99 lakh. Prices, what changed and which variant to buy: Innova Hycross facelift launched: which one to buy now.

Toyota Kirloskar Motor has released the first teaser for the facelifted Innova Hycross. The launch is set for Wednesday, 25 November 2026. This is the first major update to the MPV since it went on sale in India in November 2022, and the timing matters if you are about to book one.

If You Are Booking This Week

Ask the dealer in writing whether your booking will be billed as the current model or the facelift, and at which price. A week’s wait costs little. Taking delivery of the outgoing model makes sense only if the price or the delivery date clearly works in your favour.

What the Teaser Actually Confirms

Very little, so far. The teaser shows a new pair of headlamps with LED daytime-running-light signatures similar to those on the Toyota Taisor. That is the only design change Toyota has shown.

Everything else being reported about the facelift is an expectation from the motoring press, not a Toyota announcement. We have split the two below so you can see which is which.

ChangeStatus
New headlamps with Taisor-style LED DRLsConfirmed in teaser
New grille, bumpers, tail-lamps and alloy wheelsExpected
12.3-inch touchscreen and 12.3-inch digital driver display (as on the updated Indonesian model)Expected
New colours and upholsteryExpected
Flex-fuel version of the 2.0-litre strong hybridExpected
PriceNot announced; a small premium over the current model is expected

The Flex-Fuel Hybrid Is the Big Unknown

Autocar India reports that the facelift is likely to be the first flex-fuel strong hybrid to launch in India, using the same 2.0-litre four-cylinder hybrid system with flex-fuel capability added. Toyota has not said which ethanol blends it will be certified for, or whether the non-hybrid petrol versions will accept blends beyond E20.

For a buyer, flex-fuel capability is useful only if higher-ethanol fuel is sold where you drive. Wait for the certified blend in Toyota’s launch specification before treating it as a reason to pay more.

Current Prices, for Reference

The current Innova Hycross is priced from ₹19.53 lakh to ₹31.84 lakh ex-showroom, according to Autocar India. Expect the facelift to cost a little more, though nothing is official until 25 November.

The Maruti Suzuki Invicto is a badge-engineered sibling of the Hycross. It is not yet clear whether it will receive the same update, so Invicto shoppers face the same question with even less information.

Wait or Buy Now?

Wait if the new look and screens matter to you, if you are buying a top variant where feature updates usually land, or if flex-fuel capability is part of your plan.

Consider the current model if you need the car now and the dealer offers a clear written deal on outgoing stock. An outgoing model can sometimes be negotiated harder, but the discount is not automatic, so ask for it on paper.

This is a pre-launch report. We will update it with confirmed prices and specifications after 25 November.

FAQs

When is the Innova Hycross facelift launching?
Toyota has scheduled the launch for Wednesday, 25 November 2026.

Will the Hycross facelift be flex-fuel?
It is expected, not confirmed. Reports say the 2.0-litre strong hybrid may gain flex-fuel capability, but Toyota has not announced the certified ethanol blend.

How much does the current Innova Hycross cost?
Autocar India lists the current model at ₹19.53 lakh to ₹31.84 lakh ex-showroom. The facelift is expected to cost slightly more.
