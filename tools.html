Buying Tools

Four quick calculators for the money side of buying and running a car. Every box starts with an example value. Replace it with your own loan offer, local fuel price and mileage for an answer that fits you.

Nothing you type leaves your browser. The results are estimates, not quotes.

  • EMI Calculator
  • Fuel Cost
  • Petrol vs CNG
  • Ownership Cost

EMI Calculator

Estimate the monthly payment on a car loan.

Uses the standard reducing-balance formula: EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1), where P is the loan, r the monthly rate and n the number of months. Processing fees, insurance bundled into the loan and pre-payment charges are not included.

Fuel Cost Calculator

What your driving costs in fuel each month and each year.

Cost = distance ÷ mileage × fuel price. For an EV, put your efficiency in km per kWh and your electricity cost per kWh. Our guide on claimed vs real-world EV range explains why real figures differ from claims.

Petrol vs CNG

How much CNG saves you each month, and how long the extra price of the CNG version takes to pay back.

Payback = extra price ÷ monthly fuel saving. It ignores the smaller boot most CNG cars have, extra CNG-kit servicing and the hydro-testing of the cylinder. For a worked example with real Brezza figures, read Brezza petrol vs CNG: the break-even point.

Ownership Cost

A simple estimate of what a car costs you over several years: the car, the loan interest, fuel, insurance and servicing.

Total = on-road price + loan interest + fuel + insurance + servicing. It does not include depreciation (what the car loses in resale value), tyres, batteries, repairs, parking or tolls. For one model worked through in detail, see Brezza maintenance cost: 5-year estimate.

These calculators only do arithmetic on the numbers you enter, so the answer is only as good as those numbers. Prices, rates and mileage change: check them with the dealer, your lender and your fuel station. Want help choosing a car first? Try the car finder on our homepage.
