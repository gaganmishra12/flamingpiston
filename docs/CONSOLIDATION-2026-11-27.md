**`build-lists.js` in detail.** Everything up to the selection step comes from C:
- reading articles (`datePublished` with time)
- registering new articles as Priority 3
- skipping drafts and future-dated stories
- sorting by original timestamp, then URL
- choosing the lead: `hero.article` inside its dates, else the newest Priority 1 hero-eligible story with a hero banner, else the current lead, else Priority 2 as a last resort
- Compare, Car Culture and New & Upcoming (curated) selection
- the `featurable` rule (live, not excluded, not unverified)

From there B's rendering takes over for HERO, STORIES, FEATURED, COMPARE, UPCOMING, REVIEW and GUIDES. Two rules were added to join them:
- **One `CATEGORY` definition**, used by both the Latest Stories tabs and the article catalogue: the registry `category` override, else the listing page the story is on, else its type. This removes the two slightly different classifications that B and C each had.
- **Latest Reviews and Guides** skip stories already used by the lead, the banner modules or the default (All) Latest Stories tab, with C's fallbacks when every candidate is already shown.

C's supporting-story lanes and the 2:1 feed list were removed. The article catalogue now records `lead`, `compare`, `car-culture`, `new-launch`, `upcoming`, `latest-stories`, `latest-review` and `guides`.

**Single sources of truth:**
- Article HTML: title, description, dates, images and credit.
- `data/articles.json`: id, priority, hero eligibility, status, category override, subtype and vehicles.
- `data/homepage.json`: curation, crops and banners.
- `data/cars.json`: prices.
- `data/article-catalog.json` is generated only.
