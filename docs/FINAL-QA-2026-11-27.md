## 2. Publication approval rules (corrected)

**The problem.** The previous build listed the rewritten Innova Hycross guide publicly while it was still "unverified". Its rewrite had replaced the approved page.

**The fix.** Publication and homepage promotion are now two separate questions.

- **Publication status** (`data/articles.json` `status`):
  - `published` means approved and public.
  - `draft` and `review` are **not public**: they are not listed anywhere, and their URL answers 404.
  - `unverified` is kept as an alias of `review`.
- **Homepage promotion** is controlled by `exclude`, `priority` and `hero`. A published story can be kept off the homepage. A story that is not public is never anywhere.
- **Pending revisions of published articles** live in `drafts/<same path>`. The public page, listings, sitemap, RSS and search keep the approved version. Each build lists them (`Waiting in drafts/ for approval`), and the catalogue marks `revisionPending`.
- **Innova Hycross:**
  - The public guide and teaser are **back to the approved versions** that are live now, with identical content, URLs and SEO metadata.
  - The fact-checked rewrite (with the 80 km/h and Safety Sense edits) and the teaser's launch update wait in `drafts/`.
  - Both stay in `exclude`, and the registry shows them as `published`, Priority 2 and 3.
  - **To approve:** copy each file from `drafts/` over its public file, delete the drafts copy, rebuild, and deploy. Also remove the guide from `exclude` and add a clean `thumb` once its new hero image exists.
- **Documentation:** `docs/PUBLISHING.md` §5 has one table and the workflows for every case: draft, needs verification, scheduled, approved, published but excluded, and pending revision.

## 3. Direct-URL protection (static site, no backend)

Before this change, a draft or scheduled article was left out of listings but could still be opened by its direct URL. Now:

- **`drafts/` is blocked whole** by the existing internal-files rule in `.htaccess`, alongside `docs/`, `data/`, `scripts/` and `templates/`.
- **Generated 404 rules.** Every article file at a public path that is `draft`, `review` or still scheduled gets its own `RedirectMatch 404` rule.
  - `build-lists.js` writes these between `# FP:UNPUBLISHED:START` and `# FP:UNPUBLISHED:END` at every rebuild.
  - A rule is removed by the first rebuild after the story goes live. Today the block is empty.
- **Tested on Apache 2.4.58** with the real `.htaccess` (`mod_alias`, `mod_rewrite`, `AllowOverride All`), on the throwaway test copy and on the consolidated build:

| URL type | Result |
|---|---|
| Published article | 200 |
| Draft | 404 |
| Story in review | 404 |
| Scheduled story, before its time | 404 |
| Scheduled story, after a later rebuild | 200 |
| `drafts/…` | 404 |
| `data/`, `docs/`, `scripts/` | 404 |
| Hycross guide and teaser | 200, approved versions |

Hostinger shared hosting normally runs LiteSpeed, which reads the same `.htaccess` directives. After deploying, open one blocked URL (for example `/data/articles.json`) to confirm it returns 404 on the live server.

**Finding to fix later (existing behaviour, not changed).** On stock Apache, the existing rule `Redirect 301 /index.html /` also catches the internal DirectoryIndex lookup for `/`. Apache answers `/` with a 301 to itself, which is a redirect loop. The live site on Hostinger/LiteSpeed doesn't show this. CLAUDE.md says to keep existing redirects, so I left it as is. If you ever move to plain Apache, replace it with:

```
RewriteCond %{THE_REQUEST} \s/index\.html[\s?]
RewriteRule ^index\.html$ / [R=301,L]
```

That form works on both servers. It needs your approval.

## 4. Hero images for Priority 1 stories

`docs/PUBLISHING.md` §6 sets the rule: a Priority 1 story needs a dedicated hero banner before it can lead. The banner must meet these requirements:

- 2.4:1
- whole car framed on the right
- no text, logos or watermarks
- not distorted
- ≥1600 px wide after cropping
- provenance credited, and AI illustrations labelled

If no suitable image exists, the build keeps the current valid lead and names the story in its `NOTE` line. It also refuses a chosen lead that has no banner. Both cases are covered by tests.

## 5. Test results

**Publishing automation: `python3 scripts/test-publishing.py`, 40/40 passed.** It runs on a temporary copy, which is deleted afterwards.

- **All five kinds:** a news story, Buyer's Brief, comparison, buying guide and Car Culture story each:
  - are registered automatically as Priority 3
  - become the newest item on their Latest Stories tab (the Car Culture story also appears under News, as Car Culture news should)
  - appear on their listing page: news, reviews, comparisons, guides or car-culture
  - appear in the sitemap, RSS and search, with the correct category and placement in the catalogue
- **Draft, review and scheduled stories:** absent from the homepage, listings, sitemap, RSS and search; 404 by direct URL on Apache. The scheduled story goes live (listed, 200) after a rebuild past its time, and its rule is removed.
- **Pending revision:** the public page, listings, homepage, sitemap, RSS and search keep the approved version; `drafts/` returns 404; the build and catalogue report it. Copying it over the public file publishes it at the next rebuild.
- **Hero selection:**
  - A chosen lead within its dates leads, even when it's an older news story.
  - After its end date it falls back automatically, with a WARN.
  - A Priority 1 news story can lead.
  - `hero:false` is respected.
  - A missing hero banner never displaces a valid lead, and a chosen lead without a banner is refused.
  - Routine Priority 3 stories never lead.
- **Other rules:** exclusions keep a story off the homepage but on its listing page; editing an old article doesn't move it up; a second identical rebuild changes nothing, RSS and `.htaccess` included.
