Yangwang Altior Revealed: BYD’s Coach-Door Limousine Is Sized Between a Maybach and a Phantom

AI illustration: FlamingPiston
Gagan Mishra
Published Nov 21, 2026
3 min read
Category: News
Share

Yangwang, the luxury brand of China’s BYD, revealed the Altior in Abu Dhabi on 7 November 2026. It is a four-seat chauffeur limousine, 5,600 mm long, with rear coach doors and a claimed range of more than 1,000 km on China’s CLTC cycle. The first batch is a limited edition of 399 cars, now open for pre-orders in China. It has not been announced for India, but it shows how far up the market BYD now wants to go, in the same month its Denza brand went on sale here.

Why an Indian Buyer Should Care

You can’t buy the Altior in India, and no plan has been announced. It matters as a marker: BYD now wants to compete at the very top of the luxury market. If you are weighing a Denza here, watch how this car is received in China and the Gulf.

“Confirmed” here means two independent outlets, CnEVPost and electrive, agree on it. We could not reach an official BYD or Yangwang release, so treat every figure as provisional until one is published.

Electric or Hybrid? Not Settled Yet

electrive describes the Altior as fully electric. CnEVPost reports that Yangwang has not said whether it is a hybrid or a pure electric car. Until Yangwang states it plainly, we are not calling it either.

The range claim needs the same care. CLTC is China’s official test cycle, and electrive calls it “generous”. A figure measured on it will usually be higher than what the car manages on a real motorway trip. Our explainer on why a claimed EV range rarely matches a road trip shows why.

Where It Sits Among Flagship Limousines

On size alone, electrive puts the Altior somewhere between the Rolls-Royce Phantom and the Mercedes-Maybach S-Class. That is the company it is chasing: chauffeur-driven buyers who sit in the back. With only four seats, the rear is two individual chairs rather than a bench, and the doors open in opposite directions for easier entry. It gets a Dynaudio sound system with 39 speakers and rides on 21-inch wheels.

electrive says the car is aimed mainly at China and the Middle East; its world debut was in Abu Dhabi. BYD has not confirmed plans for Europe.

What It Means for India

There is no Indian price, date or plan for the Altior, and nothing in the reports suggests one is coming soon. The BYD luxury product you can buy here today is from Denza, which went on sale in India on 13 November 2026 with the D9 MPV and Z9 GT. Our Denza launch story has the prices.

For Indian readers, the Altior is a signal rather than a shopping option. BYD is testing whether buyers in the most expensive part of the market will accept a Chinese badge. If you are cross-shopping Denza against established European luxury brands, how the Altior is received in China and the Gulf is a fair early test of how BYD’s luxury brands hold their standing.

FAQs

Is the Yangwang Altior coming to India?
Not announced. The Altior is aimed mainly at China and the Middle East. BYD sells its Denza luxury brand in India, which went on sale on 13 November 2026.

What is the Yangwang Altior’s range?
Yangwang claims more than 1,000 km on China’s CLTC test cycle. The battery capacity has not been disclosed, and real-world range is usually lower than a CLTC figure.

How many Yangwang Altiors will be made?
The first limited edition, called Shanhe Dingyun, is capped at 399 cars worldwide. A five-seat version is reported to follow.
