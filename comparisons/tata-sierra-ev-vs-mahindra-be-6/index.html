Tata Sierra EV vs Mahindra BE 6: Both 5-Star, Which One to Buy?

AI illustration: FlamingPiston (based on Tata Motors and Mahindra images)
Gagan Mishra
Published Nov 22, 2026
5 min read
Category: Comparison
Share

The Tata Sierra EV and the Mahindra BE 6 both hold five-star Bharat NCAP ratings, and they start within ₹66,000 of each other: ₹18.79 lakh ex-showroom for the Sierra EV and ₹19.45 lakh for the BE 6 SPORTEQ. Safety will not decide this one for you. Body shape, space, charging and how you will use the car will.

The Short Version

Pick the Sierra EV for rear-seat space, a squarer boot and the option of all-wheel drive. Pick the BE 6 for faster DC charging, a slightly better adult crash score and the lower upfront price of Battery as a Service. We have not driven either car; this comparison uses published specifications and Bharat NCAP results.

The BE 6 scores higher for adults, and all of the adult points the Sierra EV lost came in the frontal test, where Bharat NCAP rated the driver’s feet “marginal”. Our Sierra EV crash-test report has the full breakdown.

There is one caveat on the other side. The BE 6 was rated in January 2025, before the SPORTEQ update. The Sierra EV rating is new and covers every Sierra EV variant. Both cars come with six airbags and stability control as standard.

Neither price includes a home charger. Tata charges ₹49,000 for its 7.2 kW charger with installation. Mahindra also sells the BE 6 One and Two with the battery rented (Battery as a Service): ₹11.45 lakh and ₹12.95 lakh, plus ₹3.75 per km for the battery. Our BaaS break-even guide shows when renting the battery pays off. The Sierra EV prices are its June launch prices, which ZigWheels still listed on 22 November. Tata’s own website could not be read, so confirm with a dealer.

Claimed ranges for the smaller packs differ by source. The Sierra EV 63 kWh is quoted at 535 km (Autocar India) or 565 km (CarWale, CarDekho). The BE 6 59 kWh is quoted at 548 km or 573 km. Expect less in daily use. In Autocar India’s real-world test of the 79 kWh BE 6, before the SPORTEQ update, it managed 449 km.

The Sierra EV AWD is the quicker car on paper, with a claimed 0–100 km/h time of 5.8 seconds. Mahindra claims 6.44 seconds for the BE 6 FE. If you drive long distances on highways, the BE 6’s faster charging matters more than either number.

The BE 6 is the longer and wider car, with the longer wheelbase. The upright Sierra EV is taller, and its boot is the more useful shape for luggage, though Tata’s 622-litre figure is measured to the roof. Compare it with the 450-litre figure instead. Autocar India found the BE 6’s rear headroom tight for tall passengers and its rear visibility limited, which is the price of its coupe roofline.

Which One Should You Buy?

  • Buy the Sierra EV if rear-seat adults and luggage are a regular part of your driving, if you want all-wheel drive (Empowered A 75 AWD, ₹25.99 lakh), or if the lowest outright entry price matters.
  • Buy the BE 6 if you mostly drive with one or two people, take long highway trips where 180 kW charging saves time, or want the lowest upfront cost through Battery as a Service.
  • At around ₹21 lakh, the Sierra EV Adventure brings a panoramic sunroof and a 360-degree camera. The BE 6 Two brings a three-screen cockpit, 16-speaker Harman Kardon audio and a 540-degree camera. Decide which matters more before you test-drive.

Test-drive both cars with your family in the back seat. Then charge each one once at a fast charger you actually use before you book.

FAQs

Which is safer, the Tata Sierra EV or the Mahindra BE 6?
Both have five stars from Bharat NCAP. The BE 6 scored 31.97 out of 32 for adults and the Sierra EV 30.62; both scored 45 out of 49 for children. The BE 6 was tested in January 2025, before the SPORTEQ update.

Which is cheaper, the Sierra EV or the BE 6?
The Sierra EV starts at ₹18.79 lakh ex-showroom and the BE 6 SPORTEQ at ₹19.45 lakh. With Battery as a Service, the BE 6 starts at ₹11.45 lakh plus ₹3.75 per km for the battery.

Which has more boot space?
The Sierra EV: 450 litres to the parcel shelf (622 litres to the roof) against 455 litres for the BE 6, which adds a 45-litre front boot. The Sierra EV is the taller, more upright car.
