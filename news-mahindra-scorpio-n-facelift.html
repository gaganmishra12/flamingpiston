2026 Mahindra Scorpio N Facelift Launched: Price List, Variants and What's New

AI illustration: FlamingPiston
Image: Mahindra
Gagan Mishra
Published Aug 10, 2026 · Updated Nov 23, 2026
5 min read
Category: News
Share

Mahindra launched the updated Scorpio N on 5 August 2026 at ₹13.69 lakh ex-showroom. It is a mid-life update, not a new generation: the engines, gearboxes and 4WD hardware carry over, the body changes are limited to new alloy wheels and two new colours, and most of the work has gone into the cabin. Mahindra revised prices on 6 October, so the launch price list no longer holds for every variant. Below are the current prices, what changed and which trims get what, as of 23 November 2026.

At a glance

  • Price: ₹13.69–25.49 lakh ex-showroom (November 2026), after a revision of up to ₹19,499 on 6 October.
  • New: panoramic sunroof, 12.3-inch touchscreen, 10.25-inch digital instrument cluster, 540-degree camera, new 18-inch alloys and two new colours.
  • Unchanged: 2.0 turbo-petrol and 2.2 diesel engines, manual and automatic gearboxes, diesel 4WD.
  • Not new: Level 2 ADAS (on the Z8L since July 2025) and the Z8S and Z8T trims.

The cheapest Scorpio N is the Z2 petrol manual at ₹13.69 lakh; the cheapest diesel is the Z2 manual at ₹14.19 lakh. The cheapest automatic is the Z4 petrol at ₹17.16 lakh, and the cheapest 4WD is the Z4 diesel manual at ₹18.02 lakh. The range tops out with the Z8L 7-seat diesel 4WD automatic at ₹25.49 lakh.

The 6 October revision raised some prices by up to ₹19,499. The biggest increase went to the Z8L 6-seat diesel automatic with ADAS, according to V3Cars and CarDekho. Compared with the launch list, several variants, mostly diesels, are now about ₹10,000 dearer. The starting and top prices did not change. Prices are as listed by CarWale, CarDekho and V3Cars on 23 November; Mahindra’s own site confirms the ₹13.69–25.49 lakh range.

What’s new in the 2026 Scorpio N

Cabin and features

  • Panoramic sunroof: a two-pane roof replaces the single-pane sunroof on the top variants. Lower trims keep the single-pane unit.
  • Bigger screens: a 12.3-inch floating touchscreen and a 10.25-inch digital instrument cluster on higher variants, with a reworked dashboard and slimmer AC vents below the screen.
  • 540-degree camera: a surround-view camera system with a blind-view monitor.
  • Charging: 65W USB Type-C ports up front.

Exterior

A new five-spoke design for the 18-inch diamond-cut alloy wheels is the only styling change CarDekho could find; the lights, grille and tail are as before. Two colours are new: Galaxy Grey and Oceanic. They join Everest White, Deep Forest and Stealth Black.

What carries over

Features from the pre-facelift car remain, including dual-zone climate control, wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay, a 12-speaker Sony sound system, and ventilated front seats on higher trims. Level 2 ADAS is still limited to the top Z8L, as it has been since July 2025. It includes automatic emergency braking, lane-keep assist and forward collision warning; adaptive cruise control comes only with the automatic.

Engines, gearboxes and 4WD

Nothing has changed mechanically. The automatic is a torque converter on both engines, and 4WD is offered only with the diesel, with either gearbox. Figures are from CarDekho and V3Cars; Autocar India quotes the same engines as 204 hp, 132 hp and 175 hp.

Safety and size

Six airbags are fitted only on higher trims; CarDekho notes that lower variants still have two. Electronic stability control and hill-descent control are available. The Scorpio N’s crash rating is still the 2022 Global NCAP result, under the older protocol: five stars for adult occupants and three stars for children. The car has not been rated by Bharat NCAP.

V3Cars lists the Scorpio N at 4,662 mm long, 1,917 mm wide and 1,870 mm tall, with a 2,750 mm wheelbase and 187 mm of ground clearance. It is sold with six or seven seats, and the standard warranty is 3 years or 1,00,000 km.

What’s next

Reported, not yet announced: a Scorpio N Edition One was seen at a dealer yard in November 2026, according to CarWale and Autocar India, with a new grille, D-pillar badging and a dark red-and-black interior. Mahindra has not announced it or its price. For Mahindra’s August 2026 event, see our Mahindra Independence Day 2026 coverage.

FAQs

What is the price of the 2026 Mahindra Scorpio N?
The Scorpio N costs ₹13.69 lakh to ₹25.49 lakh ex-showroom in November 2026. The Z2 petrol manual is the cheapest and the Z8L 7-seat diesel 4WD automatic is the most expensive. Mahindra revised some prices by up to ₹19,499 on 6 October 2026.

What is new in the Scorpio N facelift?
A panoramic sunroof on the top variants, a 12.3-inch touchscreen and 10.25-inch digital instrument cluster on higher variants, a 540-degree camera, new 18-inch alloy wheels, 65W USB Type-C ports up front and two new colours. The engines are unchanged.

Does the Scorpio N facelift have ADAS?
Yes, Level 2 ADAS, but only on the top Z8L, which has offered it since July 2025. Adaptive cruise control comes only with the automatic gearbox.

Which Scorpio N variants have 4WD?
Only diesel versions: the Z4 with the manual gearbox, and the Z8T and the Z8L 7-seater with either gearbox. The cheapest 4WD is the Z4 diesel manual at ₹18.02 lakh. The petrol is rear-wheel drive only.

Did the Scorpio N’s engines change with the facelift?
No. It keeps the 2.0-litre turbo-petrol (203 PS) and the 2.2-litre diesel (132 PS or 175 PS), with 6-speed manual or torque-converter automatic gearboxes.

Prices and specifications checked 23 November 2026 against Mahindra’s website, CarDekho, CarWale, V3Cars and Autocar India. Where these sources differ, we say so. We have not driven the updated car.
