Mahindra Scorpio N Facelift Reaches Dealerships With New Features

Official photos coming soon — see Mahindra's press newsroom for launch imagery
G
Gagan Mishra
Published Aug 2026
Category: News 3 min read

Mahindra has begun rolling out an updated Scorpio N to dealerships, with the refreshed SUV bringing a mix of comfort, safety, and cabin-tech upgrades over the outgoing model. The changes are largely feature-driven rather than a mechanical overhaul, keeping the existing engine and drivetrain line-up intact.

The headline addition is a panoramic sky roof, giving the cabin a noticeably more open feel than the standard sunroof it replaces. Mahindra has also fitted a 540-degree surround view camera system with a dedicated blind-view monitor, aimed at making tight parking and low-speed manoeuvring easier to judge. On the outside, new R18 diamond-cut alloy wheels are meant to reinforce the SUV's bulkier, more road-dominant stance.

Inside, the centre fascia now houses a larger floating touchscreen — roughly 12.3 inches — paired with a redesigned digital instrument cluster offering three selectable layouts: normal, information-focused, and a stripped-back minimal view. Mahindra has kept a row of physical buttons alongside the touchscreen for climate and shortcut functions, a deliberate choice for quicker access while driving.

The update lands alongside a busier month for Mahindra more broadly — the brand is also expected to use its Independence Day event around August 15 to showcase the Vision S, a new SUV built on its NU_IQ platform that's designed to support both combustion and electric powertrains.

Why It Matters

The Scorpio N has been one of Mahindra's strongest sellers since launch, and this update is squarely aimed at keeping it feeling current against newer rivals — without touching the pricing or engine line-up that's made it popular in the first place. Expect Mahindra to confirm official pricing and variant-wise availability shortly.

Sources: Mahindra press newsroom; Autocar India; CarLelo.
