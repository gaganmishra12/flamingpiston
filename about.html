Who we are

About FlamingPiston

FlamingPiston is an independent Indian car-buying site run by Gagan Mishra from New Delhi. It reads the price list, the variant sheet and the crash report, and tells you which version of a car to actually buy, and what the brochure leaves out.

Our approach

We read the fine print so you don’t have to.

Most car coverage tells you everything about a car. We try to answer one decision well: which engine, which variant, which fuel, and what it really costs to own. Every price and specification is checked against the manufacturer’s own documents, and when we use another publication’s driving impressions, we say so and name them.

FlamingPiston is a one-person publication. Gagan researches, writes and updates every article. We don’t take payment from manufacturers or dealers for coverage, and we haven’t yet driven most of the cars we write about; each article says clearly whether we have. See How We Assess Cars and our Editorial Policy.
