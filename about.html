Who we are

About FlamingPiston

FlamingPiston is an independent Indian car-buying publication based in New Delhi. We read the price list, the variant sheet and the crash report, and tell you which version of a car to actually buy, and what the brochure leaves out.

Our approach

We read the fine print so you don’t have to.

Most car coverage tells you everything about a car. We try to answer one decision well: which engine, which variant, which fuel, and what it really costs to own. Every price and specification is checked against the manufacturer’s own documents, and when we use another publication’s driving impressions, we say so and name them.

That means every article picks a side. If the extra ₹1 lakh for a higher variant buys features most owners will rarely use, we say so. If a cheaper version is the smarter buy, we name it, and we always point out the catch: the missing safety feature, the automatic that only comes on the top trim, the mileage figure that won’t survive city traffic. Alongside that buying advice, we report new launches and reveals in India and, in Car Culture, the performance cars and stories from around the world worth knowing about.

FlamingPiston was founded by Gagan Mishra, who leads its editorial work. We don’t take payment from manufacturers or dealers for coverage, and we haven’t yet driven most of the cars we write about; each article says clearly whether we have. See How We Assess Cars and our Editorial Policy.
