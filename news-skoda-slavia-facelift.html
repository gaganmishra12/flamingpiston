Skoda Slavia Facelift: October Prices Start at ₹9.99 Lakh

Category: News
Share

Skoda announced Slavia facelift prices on 6 October 2026, following its August unveiling. The range starts at ₹9,99,900 and reaches ₹18,84,000 ex-showroom. The distinction matters: the August reveal was not the final price announcement.

The update retains the sedan’s familiar proportions while refreshing the lighting, wheels and cabin. Equipment differs by trim; examine the selected variant rather than assuming the launch car’s features are standard.

The 1.0 TSI offers a six-speed manual or new eight-speed torque-converter automatic. The 1.5 TSI uses a seven-speed DSG. Buyers can now compare a confirmed gearbox and price combination instead of the earlier estimates.

Why It Matters

With prices available, the useful comparison is the exact manual or automatic trim against the Virtus, City and Verna. Obtain an itemised quotation and drive the gearbox you intend to buy.

October Update: Prices Announced

The August unveiling is now followed by the October price announcement. Deliveries were scheduled to begin the following week. Our previous wording incorrectly treated the reveal as the completed price launch. See the updated Slavia review and Slavia–Virtus comparison.

Sources checked 16 October 2026: Skoda Auto India, 6 October 2026. Launch prices and claimed specifications are not an independent road test.
