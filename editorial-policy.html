Editorial Policy

Last updated: August 2026

FlamingPiston exists to give readers honest, practical automotive information they can actually rely on before spending their money. This policy explains how we produce our content and how we protect our editorial independence.

Editorial Independence

Our reviews and ratings are based on our own research and analysis. Manufacturers, dealers, and advertisers do not get to see or approve our reviews before publication, and cannot pay for a favorable rating.

How We Research

Our reviews are based on publicly available manufacturer specifications, official press materials, and published comparisons with similar vehicles in the same category. Where we have not personally test-driven a vehicle, we state this clearly in the article.

Affiliate Links & Advertising

Some articles contain affiliate links or advertising. This never influences our editorial opinions or ratings. See our Affiliate Disclosure for details.

Updates & Corrections

Vehicle specifications and pricing change over time. We periodically review and update older articles, and clearly mark when an article was last updated. See our Corrections Policy.
