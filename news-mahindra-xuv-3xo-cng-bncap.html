Mahindra XUV 3XO CNG Gets a 5-Star Bharat NCAP Rating: How It Compares With the Brezza CNG

AI illustration: FlamingPiston
Gagan Mishra
Published Nov 18, 2026
3 min read
Category: News
Share

The CNG version of the Mahindra XUV 3XO, sold as the XUV 3XO ZNG, has received a five-star rating from Bharat NCAP. It scored 29.36 out of 32 for adult occupant protection and 45 out of 49 for child occupant protection. Mahindra says that makes it the first sub-compact CNG SUV in India to earn five stars.

What This Means for Buyers

CNG no longer means giving up a crash-test rating in this segment. The real choice between the XUV 3XO CNG and the Brezza CNG now comes down to performance, boot space and running cost, not safety.

How the Rating Was Given

The petrol and diesel XUV 3XO already held five stars from November 2024. For the CNG model, Bharat NCAP re-ran the frontal test on an AX5 CNG car, which scored 13.91 out of 16. The rating keeps the earlier frontal score of 13.36, and the side and pole results carry over because the structure and safety systems are unchanged.

Every version gets six airbags, stability control and ISOFIX child-seat mounts as standard.

Price and Powertrain

Mahindra launched the ZNG in October, with bookings and deliveries from 20 October. Ex-showroom prices from Mahindra’s launch release:

REVX A₹12.27 lakh

The 1.2-litre turbo-petrol makes the same 82 kW and 200 Nm on CNG as on petrol, which is unusual for a CNG car. Mahindra quotes 0–100 km/h in 11.8 seconds on CNG and a certified 24.7 km/kg. Two CNG cylinders sit under the boot floor, and the car switches to petrol when the gas runs low or if its leak sensor trips.

XUV 3XO CNG vs Brezza CNG

The Brezza CNG is the obvious rival, and both now have five-star Bharat NCAP ratings. These are claimed and certified figures, not our own tests.

The Brezza CNG costs less to start, carries more luggage and claims better efficiency. The XUV 3XO CNG keeps its turbo-petrol performance on gas and scores higher for child protection. If you drive long distances fully loaded, the Brezza’s boot and efficiency count for more. If you want a CNG car that does not feel slow, the Mahindra is the one to test drive.

Brezza figures come from our Brezza CNG guide and Brezza safety explainer. To work out whether CNG pays off for your mileage, see our petrol vs CNG break-even guide.

FAQs

What is the XUV 3XO CNG Bharat NCAP rating?
Five stars, with 29.36 out of 32 for adult protection and 45 out of 49 for child protection.

What is the XUV 3XO CNG price?
Mahindra lists it from ₹9.99 lakh for the MX Edge to ₹12.27 lakh for the REVX A, ex-showroom.

Does the XUV 3XO lose power on CNG?
Mahindra says no: the 1.2-litre turbo-petrol makes the same 82 kW and 200 Nm on CNG as on petrol.
