New Audi Q3 Debuts in India, Launching This Festive Season

Image: FlamingPiston
G
Gagan Mishra
Published Aug 25, 2026
4 min read
Category: News
Share

The third-generation Audi Q3 has made its India debut at an event in Mumbai, and Audi India has confirmed the entry-luxury SUV will go on sale during the 2026 festive season. It will lead a fresh wave of Audi launches, followed by the A5 sedan in the first half of 2027 and the Q9 flagship SUV later that year.

This is a full generational change, not a facelift. The new Q3 rides on updated underpinnings, grows slightly in size, and brings a comprehensively redesigned exterior and a far more tech-focused cabin. It continues to square up against the BMW X1 — now also available in long-wheelbase form — and the Mercedes-Benz GLA.

Engine: 204hp Petrol With Quattro AWD

For India, the Q3 is expected to stick with Audi's familiar 2.0-litre TFSI turbo-petrol, producing around 204hp, paired with a 7-speed dual-clutch automatic and Quattro all-wheel drive. Audi has indicated the India car will be petrol-only, even though the Q3 is offered abroad with mild-hybrid, plug-in hybrid and diesel options. Reported performance for this powertrain includes a 0-100kph time of roughly 7.1 seconds. One point worth flagging honestly: while most reports cite 204hp, at least one outlet has quoted a lower ~188bhp state of tune, so the exact India figure is best treated as provisional until Audi confirms it at launch.

Design: Sharper, More Distinctive

The new Q3 looks noticeably more modern than the car it replaces. Up front is a larger single-frame grille with a wide honeycomb air dam, sharper headlamps and a more sculpted bonnet. Higher trims get advanced Matrix LED headlamps with micro-LED modules and segmented DRLs. At the rear, the highlight is a set of digital OLED tail-lamps joined by a full-width light bar, along with dual-tone alloy wheels, body-coloured cladding and illuminated Audi rings. It's an evolution of the familiar Q3 silhouette rather than a reinvention, but the detailing makes it clearly a new car.

Interior & Features

Inside, the Q3 moves to a more driver-focused, digital-heavy layout. Expected highlights include a large central touchscreen (reported at up to 12.8 inches), a digital instrument cluster, a 360-degree camera, panoramic sunroof, three-zone climate control and wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay. Boot space is quoted at 488 litres. Final India-spec equipment will be confirmed variant-by-variant at launch.

Price & Launch Timing

Audi has not announced pricing. Estimates across the industry vary fairly widely — from around Rs 50 lakh to roughly Rs 60 lakh (ex-showroom) — so the number is best treated as a range for now rather than a firm figure. Audi India brand leadership has publicly described the Q3 as a festive-season product arriving in the fourth quarter of 2026, which points to an October–December window. Audi also plans to locally assemble the Q3 in India, which could help it stay competitive on price. As always, on-road cost will depend on your state's registration and insurance charges.

Why It Matters

The Q3 is the entry point to Audi's India range, so it's the model that brings the most new buyers into the brand — which makes this launch strategically important as Audi refreshes its line-up. With the BMW X1 now offered in long-wheelbase form and the Mercedes-Benz GLA due for updates, the entry-luxury SUV segment is heating up, and a sharper, better-equipped, petrol-plus-Quattro Q3 gives Audi a strong hand. The one thing everyone's waiting on is the price, which Audi will confirm at the festive-season launch.

FAQs

When will the new Audi Q3 launch in India?
It has debuted, with the market launch confirmed for the 2026 festive season (Q4, roughly October–December). Prices come closer to launch.

What engine does it have?
A 2.0-litre TFSI turbo-petrol of around 204hp, with a 7-speed dual-clutch automatic and Quattro AWD. India gets petrol only.

How much will it cost?
Not yet announced. Estimates range from about Rs 50 lakh to roughly Rs 60 lakh ex-showroom, to be confirmed at launch.

What are its rivals?
The BMW X1 (including the new X1 LWB) and the Mercedes-Benz GLA.
