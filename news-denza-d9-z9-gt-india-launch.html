BYD’s Denza Arrives in India: D9 MPV at ₹90 Lakh, Z9 GT at ₹1.57 Crore

AI illustration: FlamingPiston
Gagan Mishra
Published Nov 18, 2026
3 min read
Category: News
Share

Denza, the luxury brand of China’s BYD, went on sale in India on 13 November 2026 with two electric cars. The D9, a seven-seat MPV, costs ₹90 lakh ex-showroom. The Z9 GT, a five-seat shooting brake, has an introductory price of ₹1,56,80,000. Both cars need a ₹10 lakh booking amount.

The Short Version

The D9 is the one most Indian luxury buyers will actually consider. It sits just above the MG M9 and well below the Toyota Vellfire. Treat the nine-minute charging claim with care, and plan your ownership around home charging.

Denza D9: What ₹90 Lakh Buys

The D9 comes in one fully loaded trim and is fully imported. It seats seven in a 2+2+3 layout and uses a 115.07 kWh LFP battery with a claimed 730 km range. That figure is on the NEDC cycle, an older test that usually runs optimistic, so expect less in daily driving. Our guide to claimed versus real-world EV range explains why.

A single front motor makes 462 hp and 365 Nm, with a claimed 0–100 km/h time of 8.5 seconds. The equipment list includes powered sliding doors for the second and third rows, ventilated front and second-row seats, a 16-speaker Devialet sound system, nine airbags and ADAS.

Prices as reported by Autocar India at launch. On paper, the D9 undercuts the Vellfire by at least ₹30 lakh. The trade-off is a brand with no ownership history in India yet, so the dealer network and resale values are unknown.

The Charging Claim, in Context

BYD claims the D9 can charge from 10% to 97% in nine minutes on a DC fast charger. Its India release and brochure do not say which charger that needs. For the Z9 GT, the same nine-minute claim comes from BYD’s European release, which cites chargers of up to 1,500 kW. Chargers that fast are not a normal part of India’s public network today.

The practical answer is the 11 kW home charger that BYD includes, with installation, with both cars. By simple arithmetic, refilling a 115 kWh battery from low at 11 kW takes around ten hours or more, which suits overnight charging.

Z9 GT, Warranty and Where to Buy

The Z9 GT uses three motors with a combined 1,156 PS and 1,210 Nm, a 122.49 kWh battery and a claimed 670 km NEDC range. BYD has not given a delivery date for it; the D9 can be delivered from launch.

Both cars come with a six-year or 1,50,000 km vehicle warranty, and an eight-year or 1,60,000 km warranty on the battery and electric powertrain. Denza is selling through dedicated dealerships in Gurugram, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Chennai and Hyderabad. If you live outside those cities, ask how servicing will work before booking.

FAQs

What is the Denza D9 price in India?
₹90 lakh ex-showroom for a single fully loaded variant. Bookings cost ₹10 lakh.

What is the Denza D9 range?
A claimed 730 km on the NEDC cycle from a 115.07 kWh battery. Real-world range will be lower.

Where can I buy a Denza in India?
Through dedicated Denza dealerships in Gurugram, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Chennai and Hyderabad.
