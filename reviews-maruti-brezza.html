2026 Maruti Suzuki Brezza Review: Price, Mileage, Features, Safety & Best Variant

The 2026 Maruti Suzuki Brezza is a significant update to one of India's most established compact SUVs. The facelift keeps the familiar practical package but adds a new 1.0-litre Boosterjet turbo-petrol engine, a stronger safety story with a 5-star Bharat NCAP rating, more cabin technology and a factory CNG option that retains useful boot space.

Quick Verdict

The Brezza remains one of the easiest compact SUVs to recommend for everyday family use. Its biggest strengths are safety, practicality, fuel efficiency, a smooth automatic option and Maruti Suzuki's extensive service network. The new turbo engine adds performance, although its lack of an automatic gearbox limits its appeal.

1

Exterior Design

The 2026 facelift brings a sharper version of the Brezza's familiar upright SUV design. Updates include a revised front grille, refreshed bumper treatment, updated LED lighting elements and redesigned alloy wheels. The changes are evolutionary rather than dramatic, so owners of the previous Brezza will immediately recognise the silhouette.

2

Interior & Comfort

The cabin focuses on usability rather than trying to look radically different. Higher variants bring a 10.1-inch touchscreen, front seat ventilation, wireless charging, rear AC vents, an electric sunroof, ambient lighting, a head-up display and a 360-degree camera. The dashboard remains familiar, but the additional equipment makes the Brezza feel considerably more complete.

3

Features & Specifications

The Brezza is available in LXi, VXi, ZXi and ZXi+ trims. Depending on variant, equipment includes six airbags, ESP, hill-hold assist, wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay, Suzuki Connect, automatic climate control, TPMS, a 360-degree camera and blind-spot-related safety technology.

Performance & Mileage

The new 1.0-litre Boosterjet turbo-petrol engine produces about 109 bhp and 170 Nm, giving the Brezza a more energetic character than the naturally aspirated engine. The established 1.5-litre petrol produces around 102 bhp and is available with a six-speed manual or six-speed torque-converter automatic. The CNG version is aimed at high-mileage users and claims around 26.9 km/kg.

Safety

Safety is one of the strongest reasons to consider the 2026 Brezza. The SUV has achieved a 5-star Bharat NCAP rating, scoring 30.41/32 for adult occupant protection and 43/49 for child occupant protection. The safety package includes six airbags, ABS, electronic stability control, hill-hold assist, ISOFIX mounts and other driver-assistance and parking features on higher variants.

6

Which Brezza Variant Should You Buy?

The LXi is the entry point for budget-focused buyers, while the VXi adds a more useful spread of everyday equipment. The ZXi is the variant we would shortlist for buyers wanting the best balance of features and price. The ZXi+ is the fully loaded choice, but at that price it is worth comparing the Brezza with larger or more feature-rich SUVs before signing the cheque.

7

Verdict

The 2026 Maruti Suzuki Brezza is not a ground-up new SUV, but it is a more complete product than before. The combination of a 5-star Bharat NCAP rating, practical cabin, strong mileage credentials, CNG option, automatic gearbox and new turbo-petrol engine makes it a very well-rounded family SUV. Its biggest weakness is that the styling and cabin architecture remain conservative compared with some newer rivals.

Who Should Buy This

  • Families wanting a safe, practical compact SUV for everyday use
  • City drivers who want a smooth automatic gearbox and easy ownership
  • High-mileage users who can benefit from the factory CNG version
  • Buyers who value Maruti Suzuki's wide service network and resale support

Who Should Avoid This

  • Buyers wanting the most premium or futuristic cabin in the segment
  • Enthusiasts who specifically want a turbo-petrol automatic combination
  • Buyers spending at the very top of the range who may prefer a larger SUV

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of the 2026 Maruti Brezza?

The 2026 Brezza starts at around ₹7.40 lakh ex-showroom and goes up to approximately ₹13.71 lakh depending on variant and powertrain.

Is the 2026 Brezza safe?

Yes. The 2026 Brezza has received a 5-star Bharat NCAP safety rating, including strong adult and child occupant protection scores.

What is the mileage of the Maruti Brezza?

Claimed efficiency is around 20 kmpl for petrol versions, while the CNG version is rated at around 26.9 km/kg. Real-world mileage will vary with traffic and driving style.

Does the Brezza have an automatic gearbox?

Yes. The 1.5-litre petrol engine is available with a six-speed torque-converter automatic. The new 1.0-litre turbo-petrol is paired with a six-speed manual.

Does the Brezza come with CNG?

Yes. The factory-fitted 1.5-litre CNG version uses a six-speed manual gearbox and retains useful boot space with its underbody CNG tank.

Which Brezza variant is best?

For most buyers, the ZXi looks like the strongest balance of equipment and price. Budget buyers can consider the VXi, while the ZXi+ is best for those who specifically want the full feature package.
