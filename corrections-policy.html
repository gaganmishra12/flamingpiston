Corrections Policy

Last updated: August 2026

We aim to be transparent when our content needs to be corrected or updated.

Types of Updates

  • Factual corrections — errors in specifications, pricing, or claims. These are corrected as soon as they're verified, and the article's "Updated" date is revised.
  • Routine updates — refreshed pricing, new trim availability, or newly released competitor comparisons, added as vehicles evolve over their model life.

How to Report a Correction

Email connect@flamingpiston.com or use our Contact page, including a link to the article and a description of the issue.

Transparency

Every article displays both its original publish date and its most recent update date in the author block at the top of the piece.
